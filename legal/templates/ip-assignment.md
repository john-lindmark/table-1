**Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement**

Dated [date], between **[Assignor name]**, of [address] ("Assignor"), and **[Company name]**, a [state] limited
liability company ("Company").

**1. Work covered.** "Work" means everything Assignor has made or makes, alone or with others, for or relating to
the Table 1 products: software in source and object form, database schemas and functions, designs and design files,
icons and logos, names, copy, documentation, test data, compiled data and rules, inventions, know-how, and all
related intellectual property rights worldwide, [for (a): made before the date of this agreement] [for (b): made
during the engagement described in Schedule A].

**2. Assignment.** Assignor assigns to Company all right, title and interest in the Work, including copyrights,
trademark rights and goodwill, trade secrets, patent rights, and the right to sue for past infringement. To the
extent any Work is a "work made for hire" under US law, Company is its author. Assignor waives, or agrees not to
assert, any moral rights where the law allows.

**3. Pre-existing and third-party material.** Assignor lists in Schedule B anything in the Work that Assignor owned
before or that belongs to someone else (including open-source software and its license, and any material produced
with AI tools). For Assignor's own pre-existing material, Assignor grants Company a perpetual, irrevocable,
worldwide, royalty-free license to use it as part of the Work.

**4. Promises.** Assignor states that, to their knowledge, the Work is their own, they have the right to assign it,
it does not knowingly copy anyone else's protected work, and they have not granted rights in it to anyone else.

**5. Help.** Assignor will sign further documents Company reasonably needs to register or enforce these rights, and
appoints Company as attorney-in-fact to sign them if Assignor can't be reached after reasonable effort.

**6. Confidentiality.** Assignor will keep Company's non-public information (source code, data, rules, plans, user
data) confidential and use it only for work for Company, during and after the engagement, and will return or delete
it when the engagement ends.

**7. Payment.** [for (a): In exchange for Assignor's membership interest in Company.] [for (b): As set out in
Schedule A; the assignment takes effect on creation and does not depend on any later payment dispute.]

**8. General.** [State] law governs. This is the whole agreement on its subject and can be changed only in writing
signed by both.
