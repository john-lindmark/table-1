# Mutual non-disclosure agreement (template)

Draft for a lawyer's review. For conversations with possible partners, stores, investors or contractors before they
see anything non-public. Anyone who will *make* something also signs `ip-assignment.md`. Fill the brackets.

---

**Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement**

Dated [date], between **[Company name]**, a [state] limited liability company, and **[Other party]**, of [address].

**1. Purpose.** The parties want to discuss [purpose, e.g. "a possible integration between Table 1 and ..."] (the
"Purpose") and may share confidential information to do so.

**2. Confidential information.** Non-public information one party (the "Discloser") shares with the other (the
"Recipient") that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including
source code, database design, data and rules, product plans, user and usage data, and business terms. It does not
include information that (a) is or becomes public without the Recipient's fault, (b) the Recipient already knew or
later gets lawfully from someone else without a duty of confidence, or (c) the Recipient develops independently
without using it.

**3. Duties.** The Recipient will (a) use confidential information only for the Purpose, (b) protect it with at
least reasonable care, (c) share it only with its own people who need it for the Purpose and are bound by duties at
least as strict, and (d) not reverse engineer anything provided.

**4. Required disclosure.** If the law requires disclosure, the Recipient will give the Discloser prompt notice
where allowed and disclose only what is required.

**5. Return.** On request, the Recipient will return or delete the Discloser's confidential information, except
routine backups, which stay under this agreement.

**6. No license, no obligation.** Nothing here transfers ownership or grants a license, and neither party must go
ahead with any deal. Feedback about a party's product may be used by that party freely.

**7. Term.** This agreement covers information shared within [2] years of its date. Duties last [3] years after
disclosure, and for trade secrets, for as long as they remain trade secrets.

**8. Remedies.** A breach may cause harm money can't fix; the Discloser may seek an injunction as well as other
remedies.

**9. General.** [State] law governs. This is the whole agreement on its subject; changes must be in writing signed
by both. Nothing here stops anyone from reporting possible violations of law to a government agency.
