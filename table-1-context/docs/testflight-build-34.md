Table 1 0.1.0 (34) — What to Test

New since build 33:

NEW TAB BAR
- Events, Collection, Insights and Account each have an icon; the tab you're on is white under a thin teal line.

FIRST LAUNCH AFTER SIGN-IN
- One screen explains why notifications and location help, with Turn on or Skip for now. The app no longer asks for notifications at sign-in, or for location as soon as Events opens. Nearby, Create event and the venue pin still ask when tapped.
- Please check: sign out and back in, try both Turn on and Skip, then confirm nothing else prompts on its own.
- Launch no longer flashes an error on Events/Account.

COMMANDER AND OATHBREAKER
- Edit → Format changes any deck's format afterwards (e.g. Modern → Commander).
- The command zone shows in every view (list, grid, stacks) as two cards side by side.
- Choosing a commander lists the deck's own fitting cards first ("In this deck"); tapping one moves that copy out of the main deck.
- Partner slot only appears for commanders that can have one, and its search offers only valid partners (Partner, Partner with, Background, Friends forever, Doctor's companion).
- Please check: an old Commander deck whose commander sits in the main deck; Thrasios + another partner; a Background commander.

SPICE
- Decks get a 0–100 Spice score (Stock / Tuned / Spicy / Brew) against tournament lists of their archetype: end of the Stats row, in Stats, and on decks shared with you. Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Pauper and Canadian Highlander. Cubes say "No cubes to compare with yet".
- Please check: a stock Modern list scores low, a brew scores high, and the spiciest cards make sense.

GROUP BY COLOR IDENTITY
- View → Group by color identity on every card list and the Deck image (Azorius · WU, Esper · WUB, Not red, Five-color, Colorless; lands by identity).
- The app rebuilds its card file once on first open to pick up identities and partner abilities.

CUBE RANKINGS
- Rankings are titled by who made them ("Mara Quinn"), with an optional Label in Settings. New ranking no longer asks for a name.

INSIGHTS
- Meta now: each deck shows Win %, Conv (top 8 rate) and 1st (event wins).
- The matchup grid sits under Meta over time with named columns; tap a square for the record.
- Meta over time is full width; the picked deck's button shows its share. Reprint-only sets no longer mark the chart.
- Example lists open like a shared deck: card art, View/Group by/Sort, Export, Deck image, card pages.

PRICES
- Cards TCGplayer doesn't price (Alpha/Beta/Unlimited Power) show Cardmarket's price in dollars, marked "≈" and "from Cardmarket" on the card page.

Send anything odd through TestFlight feedback (screenshot + shake).
