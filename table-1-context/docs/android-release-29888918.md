## What to test (internal testers)

New since the bundle of 29 October (versionCode 29888581):

NOTIFICATIONS
- Pairings, results to confirm, disputes, invitations, waitlist places, sign-ups and drops for organizers, friend requests, follows, trade wants, price alerts, cube rankings and notices from Table 1, in four groups you can turn off in Android's settings (Events, Collection, Friends, From Table 1). A tap opens the same screen as on iPhone.
- Please check: allow notifications on the screen after sign-in, then have someone pair a round or send you a friend request.

LIVE NOTIFICATIONS, WIDGETS AND TILES
- Each event you play in or run gets a live notification: your round, table, opponent and clock, with Won 2–0 / Won 2–1 or Confirm / Dispute; organizers see results in as a bar and the next step. On Android 16 they show as Live Updates.
- Home screen widgets: Next event, Collection value and Current match.
- Quick Settings tiles and long-press shortcuts on the app icon: Life tracker and Scan cards.
- Please check: add each widget, and play or run a small event with the app closed.

EVENTS
- Roto cube drafts, start to finish (Create → Roto, the draft room, Pair round 1).
- Draft seating is the long upright table with Seat the ends; finished set drafts have the Redraft pile.
- Deck photos: add one after the event; Rotate left / right before saving, and Rotate from a photo's menu after upload. Photos show whole.

SAFETY AND SHARING
- Report wherever a person appears; Who sees this on every deck, cube and binder; a confirm before anything shows to more people; View as a friend or anyone; From Table 1 notices with one appeal; Review for moderators.

INSIGHTS AND COLLECTION
- Insights picks (formats and sets you hid, format, events, window, set) follow your account across phone, web and iPhone.
- Limited loads the set on screen first.
- The Collection home shows hero art and binders straight away.
- Melee events credited in Insights ("Results from Magic Online, TopDeck.gg and Melee").

FIXES
- No error flash at launch.
- The scanner fills the screen like the iPhone's, keeps the list short, and won't read a keyboard or a list as a card.
- Google sign-in says why it failed; the email code field says "From the email".

Nothing in this bundle has been run on a phone yet (no Android device or emulator on the build Mac).
