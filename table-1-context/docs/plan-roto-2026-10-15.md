# Plan: roto (rotisserie) cube events (2026-10-15)

**Status 2026-10-16: R0 (server) built and live; R1–R4 (clients, Figma) not started.** Migration
`20261016220000_roto.sql`, events-engine v7 (round robin), client contract `contract/roto-2026-10-16.md`, checks
`supabase/tests/20_roto_test.sql` (95 passing) and `events-engine/roundrobin.test.ts`. Differences from this plan as
built: the linked deck is found by `card_lists (event_id, user_id)` (no `participants.deck_list_id`); `roto_picks.how`
also records `timeout`; drops are blocked only while picking; undo runs no auto-picks afterwards. Order follows the
project rules: server first, then iOS (Debug simulator, demo flag), web, Android in one batch, Figma after the build.

## What roto is (as we'll run it)

Every card in the cube is face up from the start. Players take turns picking **any** card still in the pool, in snake
order (1→8, 8→1, 1→8 …), until each has their picks (default 45). Nothing is hidden: everyone sees every pick. A roto
usually runs over days, not at one table, so picks happen in the app whenever it's your turn, and a player can leave a
ranked list of cards to be picked for them automatically. After the draft, players build decks from their picks and
play rounds like any other event.

## What exists today (checked 2026-10-15)

- Events: `format_name` in (`draft`, `team_draft`, `sealed`, …); `format` = `cube` when a cube is named. Cube is
  free text `cube_name` + `cube_url`; there is **no cube contents** on an event.
- Seating: `seat_event(p_event_id)` shuffles players into pods of ≤ 8 and sets `participants.seat`; status `setup` →
  `seated`. Locked once round 1 is paired.
- Cubes in Library: `card_lists` (`kind = 'cube'`) with versions and `card_list_entries (list_id, board, card_id,
  finish, qty)`. `list-import` turns a Cube Cobra link into entries with exact Scryfall printings; `cards.id` is the
  Scryfall id.
- Clients already render cards from `card_id` through the local catalog pack (name, colors, type, image), and already
  group cubes by color (White, Blue, Black, Red, Green, Multicolor, Colorless, Lands; `lists` contract "Display").
- Realtime: events broadcast through `realtime.send`; iOS/web reload on event broadcasts.
- Push (`notify-worker`) is still pending, so "it's your pick" notifications wait on it.

## Decisions

1. **New format `roto`**, limited, cube only (a set is refused), not a team format. `format` stays `cube`. Label
   "Roto · <cube name>".
2. **The pool is a snapshot.** At start the cube's cards are copied into the event, one row per copy. Editing the cube
   in Library afterwards never changes a running draft.
3. **Cube source:** one of your Table 1 cubes, or a Cube Cobra link (imported through `list-import` into your Library
   cubes first, then used, so there is one path into the pool). Recent cubes keep working as today.
4. **Pick order = seating.** Re-use `seat_event`, but roto is always **one pod with no player cap** (John,
   2026-10-16): the only limit is `pool ≥ players × picks per player`. Seat 1 picks first. Snake order by default;
   "Same order every round" is a Details option.
5. **Picks per player**: stepper, default 45, max `floor(pool / players)`.
6. **Server decides whose turn it is.** Clients send "pick this slot at pick #n"; a stale pick number is refused with
   the current state, never applied to the wrong pick.
7. **Pick list (queue).** Each player keeps a private ranked list. With **Auto-pick from my list** on (default on),
   when your turn comes the server takes the first card on your list that's still available, in the same transaction
   as the pick before it. Cards others take drop off your list. An empty list or switch off → it waits for you.
8. **Optional pick clock on an organizer schedule** (John, 2026-10-16). Off by default. When on, the organizer sets a
   time per pick for ranges of rounds, so the draft speeds up deeper in, e.g. rounds 1–10: 4 h, 11–30: 2 h, 31–45:
   30 min. Ranges are contiguous from round 1 to the last round; times from 30 s to 24 h. Editable during the draft
   (applies from the next pick). The organizer can **Pause clock** / **Resume clock**.
   - Your clock starts when your turn starts. On timeout the server picks the first available card on your list
     (`how = 'timeout'`). With nothing on your list it doesn't guess: the draft waits on you, your cell shows
     `OVERDUE`, and the organizer can Pick for you.
   - The organizer can also **Pick for** a player (audited) and **Undo last pick** (audited) at any time.
   - "Your pick" push waits on notify-worker; until then the summary line and the Live Activity carry it.
9. **Your picks fill a deck in your Library as you draft** (added 2026-10-16). When the draft starts, every player
   with an account gets a deck "<cube name> roto · <date>" (format `Cube`) in Collection → Decks, and each pick lands
   in it at once (Main deck), auto-picks and organizer picks included. The player builds by moving cuts to Sideboard;
   later picks still land in Main. Undo takes the card back out. The deck is an ordinary Library deck (versions,
   grid, edit) linked to the event, so the event shows "Your deck" and the deck shows "From <event>".
   - Guests by name can play (John, 2026-10-16): they pick once they've claimed their seat through the claim link;
     until then the organizer picks for them (and their timeouts wait, since they have no list). They get a deck
     only once they have an account; claiming the seat creates it from their picks so far.
   - The cards belong to the cube's owner, not the player, so an event deck **never shows Missing** and nothing is
     added to or taken from anyone's owned cards.
   - Deleting the deck in Library only unlinks it; the picks stay in the event.
10. **Matches after the draft** (added 2026-10-16): the event moves to the normal Live phase, and results are recorded
   exactly as in every event today (single entry, opponent confirms or disputes, auto-confirm at round end, organizer
   override, audited). The organizer picks the pairing at Create or in Details until round 1. Round robin is offered
   **for roto only** for now (John, 2026-10-16); other formats get it after it has been run once:
   - **Swiss** (default for 9+ players): today's pairing; Rounds stepper with the recommended count.
   - **Round robin** (default for 2–8): everyone plays everyone once. Rounds are fixed at n − 1 (even) or n (odd,
     one bye a round) and the stepper is hidden.
   - Standings are the same MTR standings in both (3/1/0, OMW/GW/OGW, 33% floor).
11. **Visibility:** players and organizers of the event see the whole draft; only your own pick list is private.

## Server (R0)

Migration `2026xxxx_roto.sql`:

- `events.format_name` check gains `roto`; `events_format_sync` maps it to `format = 'cube'`; `create_event_v2`
  accepts it (cube required) and `format_label` gives "Roto".
- `roto_drafts (event_id pk, picks_per_player int, order text check in ('snake','fixed'), pool_count int,
  source_list_id uuid null, source_version int null, started_at, finished_at, clock jsonb null, clock_paused_at
  timestamptz null, turn_started_at timestamptz)`. `clock` = `[{from_round, seconds}, …]` sorted, first `from_round`
  = 1, seconds 30–86400; null = no clock.
- `roto_pool (event_id, slot int, card_id uuid → cards, oracle_id uuid, finish text, primary key (event_id, slot))`.
- `roto_picks (event_id, pick_no int, participant_id, slot int, picked_at, picked_by uuid, how text check in
  ('player','auto','timeout','organizer'), primary key (event_id, pick_no), unique (event_id, slot))`.
- `roto_queue (participant_id, position int, slot int, primary key (participant_id, slot))` + `roto_settings
  (participant_id pk, auto_pick boolean default true)`. RLS: own rows only.
- RLS on pool and picks: readable by the event's participants and organizers; writes through RPCs only.

RPCs (security definer, every mutation writes `audit_log`):

| Call | Does |
|---|---|
| `roto_set_cube(p_event_id, p_list_id)` | Organizer, before start. Records the source cube (and its current version) on the draft; returns pool size and the max picks per player. |
| `roto_start(p_event_id, p_picks_per_player, p_order)` | Organizer. Needs seats. Snapshots the cube into `roto_pool` (one row per copy, Main only), sets `started_at`, locks seats and players. |
| `roto_pick(p_event_id, p_slot, p_pick_no)` | The player on the clock (or an organizer = Pick for). Refuses a wrong turn, a taken card, or a stale `p_pick_no` (returns state). Then runs auto-picks for following players while their list has a hit. Finishes the draft on the last pick. |
| `roto_undo(p_event_id)` | Organizer. Removes the last non-auto pick and every auto-pick after it. |
| `roto_queue_set(p_event_id, p_slots int[], p_auto_pick boolean)` | Replaces your list (taken slots dropped). If it's your turn and auto-pick is on, picks at once. |
| `roto_set_clock(p_event_id, p_clock jsonb, p_paused boolean = null)` | Organizer, any time before the last pick. Sets or clears the schedule (validated: contiguous from round 1, 30 s–24 h) and pauses/resumes. A change applies from the next turn; resuming moves `turn_started_at` on by the paused time. |
| `roto_expire(p_event_id)` | Anyone in the event, and the cron job. If the on-clock deadline has passed: picks the first available card on that player's list (`timeout`) and carries on (auto-picks, the next deadline); with no list, marks the turn overdue and does nothing else. No-op before the deadline. |
| `roto_state(p_event_id, p_after_pick int = 0)` | `{draft, seats[], on_clock {participant_id, pick_no, round, deadline, overdue}, clock, picks: [{pick_no, participant_id, slot, how, at}] after p_after_pick, my_queue, my_auto_pick}`. First load passes 0 and also gets `pool`. |

- Turn maths in one SQL function `roto_drafter(n_players, pick_no, order)` (snake: round = (pick−1)/n, index reversed
  on odd rounds), unit-checked in SQL like `seat_event`.
- Each pick sends one realtime broadcast on the event channel (`roto.pick` with pick_no), so clients fetch
  `roto_state(after)` incrementally instead of reloading the event.
- Clock: deadline = `turn_started_at` + the schedule's seconds for the current round (none while paused).
  `pg_cron` runs `roto_expire_all()` every minute for overdue drafts; clients also call `roto_expire` when their
  countdown reaches zero, so short clocks at a table don't wait for the next minute.
- Concurrency: `roto_pick` locks the `roto_drafts` row, so two quick picks and auto-pick chains apply in order.
- Demo: `is_demo` seeds for `demo_testers`: an 8-player roto partway through (pick ~120 of 360), and a finished one
  in round 3 of a round robin with results in, each with the tester's linked deck.
- Tests: snake order for 2/7/8 players, auto-pick chain across several players, stale pick refused, undo removes the
  right picks (and the deck lines), last pick finishes, non-participant can't read, a timeout picks from the list,
  a timeout with an empty list waits, the schedule switches times at its round boundaries, pause/resume keeps the
  remaining time, more than 8 players seat as one pod.

### Linked decks (Library)

- `card_lists` gains `event_id uuid null` (+ index); `participants` gains `deck_list_id uuid null`.
- `roto_start` creates the deck for each participant with a `user_id` through the same internal path as
  `list_create` (server-generated id, the per-user `library_summary` lock, a `seq` bump so `lists_changes` delivers it
  to every device). Note "Roto draft started".
- `roto_pick` (and each auto-pick) appends `{card_id, board: main, finish, delta: +1}` to the picker's deck in the same
  transaction, through the `list_edit` internals; the 10-minute rule groups a run of picks into one version.
  `roto_undo` applies `delta: −1` (from Main, else Sideboard).
- Claiming a guest seat (`participant_tokens` claim) creates the deck from their picks so far.
- `list_delete` on an event deck clears `participants.deck_list_id`.
- Missing: the Library's missing count skips lists with `event_id` set (server summary and the client mirrors).
- `lists_changes` rows carry `event_id` and the event name, so clients can show "From <event>".

### Pairing: Swiss or round robin

- `events.pairing text not null default 'swiss' check in ('swiss','round_robin')`. `create_event_v2` and
  `update_event_details` take `p_pairing` (refused after round 1 is paired; refused for team events). Round robin sets
  `number_of_rounds` from the active players when round 1 is paired and ignores `p_rounds`.
- `events-engine` `pairRound`: new `pairRoundRobin(order, roundNumber)` using the circle method on the seat order
  (pick order, so seat 1 is the fixed point; a phantom "bye" seat when n is odd). Pure function, deterministic: round
  k is always the same pairs, so re-pairing after an undo is stable.
- Drops in round robin: a dropped player's remaining scheduled matches become byes for their opponents (the MTR
  treatment of a bye: 2–0, 3 points). Players can't be added after round 1 (as today).
- The organizer can end a round robin early (End event) like any event.
- Tests (Deno, next to `pairings.test`): every pair meets exactly once for n = 2…10, one bye per round when odd,
  nobody gets two byes, drops turn into byes.
- Generic, not roto-only: any non-team event can use round robin; the Create UI offers it for roto first and for
  the other formats once checked.

## Client views

The event screen gets a **Draft** phase between Setup and Live (organizer: Setup / Draft / Live / Results; players see
Draft when it's running). Top of the phase, always visible, a one-line summary (progressive disclosure):
`ON THE CLOCK · ANNA · PICK 37/360` and `YOU PICK IN 3` (accent when it's you: `YOUR PICK`). With a clock, the
remaining time follows (`· 1:42:10`, `· OVERDUE`, `· PAUSED`); it is hidden when the clock is off.

Below it a Table 1 segmented control with four views:

1. **Grid** — the whole draft as the classic roto sheet. Columns = players in seat order (sticky header with names),
   rows = rounds (sticky left column 1…45, with a ↓/↑ marker for snake direction). Each cell: card name, thin color bar
   from the card's colors, `auto` marker for auto-picks. The next empty cell is outlined in accent; your column's
   header is accent. Tap a cell → card detail. Scrolls both ways; opens scrolled to the current round. Hairline grid,
   zero radius, as the house style.
2. **Cube** — every card in the pool, grouped by color (lists contract "Display"), with search and a filter
   `Available · All · Picked`. Picked cards stay in place with a flat `#545461` label `#23 · ANNA` (no opacity
   dimming). Tap a card → detail with **Pick** (only on your turn, confirm "Pick <name>?") and **Add to my list** /
   **Remove from my list**. A small `Listed #4` tag on cards already in your list.
3. **Picks** — one player's picks. Player picker at the top (You first, then seat order), so "your own picks" and
   "individual picks" are the same screen. Grouped by color, with counts per color, a mana-value curve row and
   creature/non-creature count. Toggle list / image grid like the deck grid. For you only: **Open deck** (your linked
   Library deck, already filled with your picks).
4. **My list** — your ranked pick order. Drag to reorder, swipe/× to remove, "Add from the cube" jumps to Cube.
   **Auto-pick from my list** switch at the top with a one-line explanation. Cards taken by others since you last
   looked show once as `Taken by ANNA (#41)` and then leave the list. Saving is debounced and sent as
   `roto_queue_set`.

Organizer extras in Draft: **Pick for <name>** (from Cube or Grid on the on-clock cell) and **Undo last pick**, each
with a native alert to confirm.

Setup changes for roto: Details shows the cube (Library cube or Cube Cobra link → import), picks-per-player stepper and
pick order (Snake / Same order). Seating is shown as a pick order list (1…n) with Re-seat; the table drawing stays for
pod drafts. **Start draft** replaces "Pair round 1" until the draft finishes.

Details (roto) also has **Pick clock**: a switch (off by default) and, when on, the schedule as rows
`ROUNDS 1–10 · 4 H`, each with a time stepper/menu, **Add a range** (splits the last range) and remove; editable
during the draft, where the organizer also gets **Pause clock** / **Resume clock**.

Create and Details (roto only): a **Pairing** segmented control (Swiss · Round robin) under Rounds. Round robin hides the Rounds
stepper and shows "7 rounds · everyone plays everyone" (count from the players so far). Prefilled from the player
count, editable until round 1.

After the last pick: the Draft phase shows `DRAFT DONE · 360 PICKS` with **Open deck** for players, and the organizer
gets **Pair round 1** (Live). From then on Live and Results are today's screens: pairings, match cards, result entry,
confirm/dispute, standings. Two additions:
- Round robin: Live shows a **Schedule** (each player's opponents by round, played rounds with scores, upcoming
  ones plain) and the standings header says `ROUND 3 OF 7`.
- The Draft views stay reachable from Live and Results (read-only), so players can look at each other's picks
  during the matches.

Library: an event deck shows `FROM <EVENT NAME>` under the title (tap → the event), no Missing counts, and a
`LIVE DRAFT` tag while picks are still arriving.

Offline: picks need the server (it decides the turn); show "Offline — picks resume when you're back". The list edits
queue locally and send on reconnect.

## Answers (John, 2026-10-16)

1. **Pod size:** no cap; one pod, limited only by the cube (decision 4).
2. **Pick clock:** optional, off by default, on an organizer schedule of times per range of rounds so it quickens
   deeper into the draft (decision 8).
3. **Auto-pick:** on by default (decision 7).
4. **Deck board:** picks land in Main; you cut to Sideboard (decision 9).
5. **Round robin:** roto only for now (decision 10).
6. **Guests:** yes, they pick once they've claimed their seat; the organizer picks for them until then (decision 9).

Still assumed (say if wrong): a timeout with an empty list waits on the player rather than picking an arbitrary card;
pairing defaults to round robin for 2–8 players and Swiss for 9+.
