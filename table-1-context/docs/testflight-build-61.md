Table 1 0.1.0 (61) — What to Test

On top of build 60 (everything in 60's notes, plus):

LIFE TRACKER
- Taps always count, even when your thumb slides. The counters drawer opens only from a pull down from the top strip of your box (by the small line).
- In an event, the tracker follows you into the next round: once round 2 is paired, it moves to your new match.
- Once your match's result is in, the tracker stops recording games. Change a result from the event instead.

DECK PHOTOS
- Once your last match is in (or you drop, or the event ends), your event screen leads with Add a deck photo.
- Long press any deck photo to copy, save or share it.
- Ten minutes after your last match, a push reminds you to add your deck photo if you haven't.

EVENTS
- Drafts with several pods show each pod as its own block in Round, Earlier, Players and Matchups.
- The Live Activity for players shows only the round clock, table and opponent (no result buttons).

EVERYWHERE
- Keyboards go away when you tap outside the box, drag a list or go back.
- Insights: a format's Events list is newest first.

Send anything odd through TestFlight feedback (screenshot + shake).
