What's new in this build

LINKS OPEN THE APP
• Tap any table-1.site link and it opens that screen in the app: an event, its standings, a join link, the life tracker, a deck, cube, binder, card, ranking, trade, a profile or Friends. People without the app still get the website.
• Join links with just a code now join from the app and open the event. If you're already in it, it just opens; if you can't join, it says why.
• Standings links open the event for its players; anyone else sees the standings page.
• Pages the app doesn't have (the privacy policy, account deletion) stay in Safari.

PLEASE TRY
Links can take up to a day after this build arrives to start opening the app. If one opens Safari, try again later, or delete and reinstall the app.
1. Send yourself a join link (Copy join link on the website's event screen) and tap it in Messages or Notes. Does it join and open the event?
2. Tap a standings link for an event you played in, then one for an event you didn't. The first should open the event, the second the standings page.
3. Open a deck on table-1.site, copy its address, and tap it on your phone. Does the deck open, and does Back lead to Decks?
4. Tap table-1.site/privacy. It should stay in Safari.
5. Sign out, tap a deck link, sign in. Does the deck open after sign-in?

Anything odd: screenshot it and send it with TestFlight's feedback.
