## The Buy screen

One screen for a deck, a cube, Wants and Missing from decks and cubes (iOS `LibraryRoute.buy`, web `/library/buy/<deck
or cube id | wants | missing>`, Android `collection/buy/{source}`). Reached from:

- **Deck / cube screen:** a **Buy** row after Add to collection (own lists, not roto decks): "N to buy · $X".
- **Wants:** the Buy on TCGplayer row (was: straight to TCGplayer).
- **Missing from decks and cubes:** the Buy missing on TCGplayer row (was: straight to TCGplayer).

Top to bottom (the thing first, nothing explaining itself):

1. **Bar** "Buy" with back.
2. **The total**, large (accent: it's the result), then one data line: `56 cards · 19 owned · 37 to buy`, and
   `12 unpriced` when copies to buy have no price.
3. **Options** (switches, titles only), each remembered per device and source family (`buy.<deck|cube|wants|missing>.<option>`):
   - **Skip cards I own** (decks and cubes; default on). Basic lands are left out while it's on (they're never
     missing).
   - **Any printing I own counts** (decks and cubes, while Skip is on; default on): a line is covered by its own
     printing and finish first, then any copy of the same card, lines in order, each copy once (the deck's own
     Missing rule).
   - **Exact printings** (default off for decks, cubes and Missing, on for Wants): on, each printing goes to its own
     TCGplayer product page (one link per printing, the Buy on TCGplayer menu); off, every card goes into one Mass
     Entry cart by name, then Optimize. A want for any printing always goes by name.
   - **Tokens** (decks; default off): the tokens the main deck, commander and sideboard make, one each, in their
     catalog printing. Tokens always go to their own product pages (Mass Entry by name isn't known to find tokens).
   - **Boards** (decks): a row "Main deck · Sideboard" opening a menu that turns each on or off: Command zone, Main
     deck, Sideboard (on by default), Considering (off).
4. **Cards to buy**, by board (Command zone, Main deck, Sideboard, Considering, Tokens), each section headed
   `Main deck · 12` with `· 7 owned` when any: the card row (art, name, set · finish) with the copies to buy. Cards
   you own every copy of aren't listed. A tap opens the card.
5. **Totals:** Price (every listed copy), Owned −$X (the copies you own, at the line's price), Total.
6. **Bottom bar:** **BUY ON TCGPLAYER** (off when nothing's to buy): builds the cart and opens it as before (one link
   straight away, more as the Buy on TCGplayer menu).

Prices are the line's printing and finish at TCGplayer market (near mint; a want for any printing: its "From" price),
summed in cents. An estimate: TCGplayer's Optimize picks the sellers.

## Rules (shared)

- `rows(lines, owned, options)`: one row per line in a picked board with copies, in line order: `qty`, `owned`,
  `buy = qty − owned`. Without Skip nothing is owned.
- `totals(rows)`: cards, owned, buy; price, owned value, total in cents; unpriced = copies bought without a price.
- `calls(rows, exact)`: the `tcgplayer_cart` calls: items `{card_id, qty}` (or `{oracle_id, qty}` for a want for any
  printing), copies summed per key in row order; tokens in a `p_products: true` call, everything else in a call with
  `p_products` = Exact printings; one call when they agree, none when nothing's bought.
- `merge(answers)`: Mass Entry parts in order, product pages by name, set and number; then the existing menu rule
  (`testdata/tcgplayer-cart/cases.json`).

## Card page

**Add to → TCGplayer** (beside Collection … Trade): the version(s) in view with the copies picked (One copy / All N),
each printing to its product page (`p_products: true`; a printing with no TCGplayer product goes by name). One link
opens straight away.

## Tags and telemetry

`p_from` = `deck`, `cube`, `wants`, `missing` or `card` (the affiliate link's `subId2`). `buy_opened` {from};
`buy_option` {from, option, on}; `tcgplayer_cart_opened` gains `from` deck / cube / card and `skip`, `any`, `exact`,
`tokens` (booleans). No card names.
