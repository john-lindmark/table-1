# Spice: how far a deck or cube is from its peers (2026-10-28)

Every deck and cube gets a **Spice** score, 0–100: how much of it decks or cubes like it rarely or never play.

## Peers

**Decks** compare with Insights' tournament lists (`t1_private.meta_decks`, MTGO and TopDeck.gg), never older than a
year. Formats with lists: Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Pauper, Canadian Highlander. Any other format: no score
(`no_data`). The main deck and commander are "the deck"; the sideboard is listed but never scored; Considering is
left out. Basic lands never count.

One list identical to the deck's main deck (the year's newest such list, then the highest id: the one a deck copied
from Insights most likely came from) is left out, so a copied list isn't compared with itself. Other identical lists
stay.

In order, the first that has **30 lists** or more:
1. `archetype`: the deck's archetype by Insights' rules (`t1_private.meta_label` on main + commander and sideboard,
   with the deck's colours), last 90 days, then last year.
2. `closest`: when the label is Other or too thin, the archetype (with 30+ lists) whose lists play the most of the
   deck: fit = Σ copies × share of its lists with the card in the main deck ÷ Σ copies. Ties: more lists, then the
   lower id. The last 90 days if any archetype there has 30 lists, else the year. Used only when fit ≥ 0.25.
3. `format`: every list of the format, last 90 days, else the year ("Nothing close in Modern").

**Singleton formats** (Canadian Highlander) skip those: `colors`, each card is compared with the year's lists whose
colours (Insights' deck colours) include the card's colour identity (colourless: every list).

**Cubes** compare with `t1_private.cube_corpus` (Cube Cobra's popular public cubes, once Cube Cobra allows it; empty
until then): the 20 cubes with the highest Jaccard overlap of non-basic Oracle ids, each weighted by its overlap
(ties: `source_id`). One identical cube is left out as for decks. Fewer than 5 cubes sharing a card: no score
(`no_cubes`). `close` is false when the nearest overlaps under 15% ("No cubes much like this one").

## Score

`r` = a card's share of peer lists playing it in the main deck (sideboard cards: in the sideboard), or the
overlap-weighted share of peer cubes holding it. Card spice `s = max(0, 1 − r / 0.25)` (0.10 for singleton formats).
**Spice = round(100 × Σ copies × s ÷ Σ copies)**, half up, over the deck's non-basic main and commander cards (a cube's
main section). Words: **Stock** 0–9, **Tuned** 10–24, **Spicy** 25–49, **Brew** 50+.

## `list_spice(p_list_id uuid, p_token text default null)` → jsonb

Read-only for the caller; `authenticated` and `anon` (anon only with a link). Who may call: the owner, anyone the
deck or cube is shared with (the same rule as `profile_cards`: blocks win), or anyone holding a live share link to
it (`p_token`). Anything else, a deleted list or an unknown id: `42501` "That's private". Never 40001/40P01.

```jsonc
{ "kind": "deck" | "cube",
  "score": 34 | null, "word": "stock" | "tuned" | "spicy" | "brew" | null,
  "reason": null | "empty" | "no_data" | "no_cubes",           // why there's no score
  "basis": { "kind": "archetype" | "closest" | "format" | "colors" | "cubes",
             "format": "modern" | null, "archetype": 12 | null, "name": "Boros Energy" | null,
             "lists": 412,                 // peer lists (cubes: peer cubes)
             "days": 90 | 365 | null,      // null for cubes
             "sources": ["mtgo", "topdeck"] | ["cubecobra"],
             "close": true | false | null, // cubes only
             "cubes": [{ "name": "LSVCube", "overlap": 0.62 }] | null } | null,   // the 5 nearest
  "cards": [{ "c": "oracle-uuid", "n": "Ancient Grudge", "section": "main" | "side", "copies": 4,
              "rate": 0.0049, "seen": 2, "of": 412 }],   // only cards under the stock line; main first, rarest first
  "stock_missing": [{ "c": "…", "n": "Seasoned Pyromancer", "rate": 0.5, "seen": 206, "of": 412 }] }   // ≤ 12
```

- `cards`: main cards then sideboard cards with `r` under the stock line (0.25, singleton 0.10), each by `seen`
  ascending, then copies descending, then name (cubes: by `rate`). `of` is the card's own peer count (singleton: the
  lists that can play it).
- `stock_missing`: non-basic cards in half or more of the peer lists (cubes: weighted half) that the deck's main and
  commander (the cube) don't hold, most played first, then name; at most 12. Empty for singleton formats and the
  `format` basis (the whole format's staples say nothing about one deck).
- Decks read `t1_private.spice_counts` / `spice_lists`: how many lists play each card (main, side) per format,
  archetype (-1 = the whole format) and window, rebuilt daily by `t1_private.spice_refresh()` (pg_cron
  `t1-spice-counts`, 04:40 UTC; the migration runs it once). Canadian Highlander reads its few lists directly.
- Cached per list, list `seq` and day (`t1_private.list_spice`): the second open of the day costs a lookup.
- Budgets (`perf_30_insights.sql`, a year of Modern, 36,000 lists): a deck in its archetype 15 ms, Other finding the
  closest archetype 25 ms, the daily counts 3 s (local 2026-10-28: 2.5 ms, 4.3 ms, 0.6 s).

## What clients show

`testdata/spice/cases.json` `display` per case is exact:

- **Stats row** summary of the deck/cube screen gains `Spice 34` at the end (` · ` before it); nothing when `score` is
  null or the call failed.
- **Stats → Spice** (decks and cubes; web `/library/list/:id/stats`): heading SPICE, `headline` ("34 · Spicy") in the
  accent, `basis` line, then **Spiciest cards** (`cards`: "4 Ancient Grudge · in 2 of 412 lists", "never seen",
  "in 3 of 40 lists that can play it", "in 3 of 20 similar cubes", "in no similar cube", " · sideboard" after a
  sideboard card), the first 8 with **Show all N**, then **Stock cards you don't play** (`missing`: "Seasoned
  Pyromancer · in 206 of 412 lists"). Cards open their card page. With no score the section shows `reason` alone:
  "Add cards to see how spicy this is", "No tournament lists for this format yet", "No cubes to compare with yet".
  Cubes list the 5 nearest cubes under the basis line ("LSVCube · 62% alike").
- Shared decks and cubes (profile, link) show the same, calling with the link's token when opened from one.
- Loaded once per screen open after the list loads; re-asked after an edit is saved. Demo: from the cases.

## Telemetry

Screen `deck_spice` / `cube_spice` (the Stats section scrolled into view or opened); event `spice_viewed {kind,
format, basis, word}` once per list per screen open, and `spice_card_opened {kind, section}`.
