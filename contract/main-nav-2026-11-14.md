## 1. The bars

The tab bar (Figma T1/Tab Bar 545:746) is unchanged in look; its labels become **Play · Cards · Insights · Account**
(the tab ids stay `events`, `collection`, `insights`, `account`; icons unchanged).

Directly above it sits the **sub-tab strip** (Figma 765:113447): one row, 44 pt/dp tall, equal cells edge to edge on
the background (`#171725`), no gaps; each cell a 9 pt mono label, uppercase, tracking 1.5, centred. The current sub-tab
is white (`text`) under a 1 pt accent line along the cell's top edge; the others are Neutral_4 (`muted`). The strip
shows exactly when the tab bar shows (a tab's root, keyboard down) and hides with it. In a side rail (landscape,
foldables) the strip runs along the bottom of the content.

| Tab | Sub-tabs (left to right) | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Play (`events`) | Events · Life tracker | Events |
| Cards (`collection`) | Collection · Trade · Wants | Collection |
| Insights (`insights`) | Constructed · Limited · Rankings | Constructed |
| Account (`account`) | Profile · Records · Settings | Profile |

Tapping a sub-tab swaps the tab's root screen in place (no push, no back arrow; each sub-tab root keeps its scroll
and state while the tab is alive). **Life tracker is the only sub-tab that opens full screen**: it opens the life
tracker over everything (no tab bar, as before) and the strip stays on Events underneath; closing the tracker comes
back to Play → Events. Telemetry: `nav_sub_tab` {tab, sub}.

## 2. Remembering

Each tab remembers its last sub-tab, kept on the device (iOS UserDefaults `nav.sub.<tab>`, Android prefs
`nav.sub.<tab>`, web localStorage `t1.nav.sub.<tab>`), so tapping a tab returns to the sub-tab (and, as before,
the screens pushed on top of it) where the person left it, across launches too. Life tracker is never remembered
(it isn't a place). A stored value that isn't one of the tab's sub-tabs reads as the default. The rule is
`MainNav.remembered(tab, stored)`; cases `remembered`.

Insights' Constructed / Limited is the same pick Insights already keeps (`section`, which follows the account,
contract/insights-2026-10-24.md § What the viewer picked): picking Constructed or Limited sets `section` and the
remembered sub-tab; Rankings is remembered on its own and leaves `section` alone. Opening Insights on a remembered
`constructed`/`limited` uses `section`.

A link, push or widget that opens a sub-tab's own screen (Trades, Wants, every ranking of yours) selects that sub-tab
instead of pushing a copy of it (cases `routes` on the web; the apps map their routes the same way). Anything deeper
pushes as before.

## 3. What each sub-tab holds

**Play → Events**: the Events home, unchanged.

**Cards → Collection**: the Collection home without its bottom Trade · Wants · Import row (the strip replaces it);
**Import** moves into the home's + menu (after New cube: "Import" → Import and export). **Trade**: the Trades screen
as a root (no back arrow). **Wants**: the Wants screen as a root (no back arrow).

**Insights**: the Constructed / Limited segmented control at the top of Insights is gone; the strip picks the section.
**Rankings** is the "every ranking of yours" screen as a root, titled Rankings, in this order:

1. **Yours**: every ranking of yours (`cube_rankings()`), newest first (empty: "No rankings yet. Rank a popular cube
   below, or open one of your cubes and tap Rankings.").
2. **Your friends'**: your friends' rankings you may open, newest first, each with its maker's avatar and the subject
   (`rankings_friends(p_limit)`, below). Empty: "When a friend shares a ranking, it shows here."
3. **Popular cubes**: PowerLSV, LSVCube, MTGO Vintage Cube, Arena Powered Cube; a tap opens the cube's rankings,
   where Rank <cube> starts one (contract/cube-rankings-2026-10-21.md § Popular cubes).
4. **Cubes you can rank**, 5. **Sets you can rank**: as before.

A client that gets an error from `rankings_friends` (older server) shows no friends' section.

**Account → Profile**: your own profile as others see it (the profile screen for your id, as a root), with Friends
(requests waiting) at its top. **Records**: the lifetime record (big), Deck photos, the record offer, then
**Head-to-head** with every opponent (no Show all cut-off). **Settings**: everything that was a setting or an
account action on the Account screen, in this order: Profile photo, Display name, Sharing, Track games, Nearby alerts,
Improve scanning (apps), Share usage and crash data, the OS settings row (apps), Payments (when open to the account),
From Table 1 / Review (moderation), Bugs (watchers), Support, Developer (testers), Account (Sign out, Delete account),
About (fan content notice, Terms, Privacy, version).

## 4. Server

`public.rankings_friends(p_limit integer default 200) returns jsonb`: an array of your accepted friends' rankings you
may open (`t1_private.ranking_seen`: shared ones; a cube ranking only when its cube reaches you; never Only me), never
your own, never one of a deleted cube, newest first, at most `p_limit` (1–500). Each row is `cube_rankings()`'s row
(`id`, `name`, `by`, `mine` false, progress fields…) with its subject under `cube`. Signed in only (28000);
authenticated only. Migration `20261114200000_rankings_friends.sql`, test `supabase/tests/80d_rankings_friends_test.sql`,
budget in `perf_10_library.sql`.

## 5. Web

Phones draw the strip above `TabNav` at the bottom; desktop (≥ 1024 px) keeps its sidebar and draws the sub-tabs as
the same strip at the top of the column. Sub-tab paths (cases `tabs[].subs[].web`): Play `/` and `/life` (full
screen), Cards `/library`, `/library/trades`, `/library/wants`, Insights `/insights`, `/insights/limited`,
`/insights/rankings` (the old `/library/rankings` still opens it), Account `/me`, `/me/records`, `/me/settings`.
A top tab links to its remembered sub-tab's path.
