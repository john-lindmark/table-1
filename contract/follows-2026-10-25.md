# Following decks, cubes and binders, and trade wants (2026-10-25)

Follow someone else's deck, cube or binder and get one push when they've finished a round of changes, not one per
card. And (§ Trade wants) a push when a friend puts cards you want up for trade. Server: migration `20261026050000_follows.sql`; tests `supabase/tests/87_follows_test.sql` and
`86_trade_wants_test.sql`; budgets in `perf_10_library.sql` (`follows_list`, `follow_digest`, `trade_want_digest`). Push copy in `supabase/functions/notify-worker/follow.ts`
(tests `follow.test.ts`). Builds on `contract/friends-sharing-2026-10-18.md` (who sees what) and
`contract/share-links-2026-10-23.md` (links).

## Decisions (John's request, 2026-10-25; the numbers are first picks, easy to change)

- **What you can follow:** any binder, deck or cube that isn't yours and that you can see, on a profile (Friends /
  Anyone, capped by the profile) or through a link you opened. Not All cards, For trade or Wants (they change with
  every trade; a follow-up if wanted).
- **You keep it only while you can see it.** Follow is checked again at every push and every read. If the owner
  narrows it, stops the link you followed through, blocks you or deletes it, it reads "This isn't available" in
  Following and sends nothing. Widening it again brings it back. The owner is never told who follows, or how many.
- **Changes are grouped.** A change starts a group; the group goes out once the item has been **quiet for 30
  minutes**, and an item sends **at most one update every 6 hours** (later changes wait and join the next one).
  What's sent is the net difference from the last update: cards added then taken out again cancel; a run of edits
  that ends where it began sends nothing.
- **What counts:** decks and cubes compare Main / Sideboard / Commander lines by card and section (Considering is
  left out, like everywhere else others see a deck); binders compare copies by printing (finish, condition and
  language moves don't count). Renames don't count.
- **Pushes are per follow** (on by default, off keeps it in Following with an "updated" mark) and can be muted
  for every follow at once with the `follow.updated` kind. Each follower's own history starts when they followed:
  earlier changes aren't shown.
- **Limits:** 500 follows per account; 100 follow changes an hour (53400). Every change is audited in
  `account_audit` (`follow.set`).

## RPCs (authenticated; anon is refused)

| Call | Does |
|---|---|
| `follow_set(p_kind, p_id, p_follow, p_push?, p_token?)` | `p_kind`: `binder` / `deck` / `cube`. Follow (`p_follow` true) or unfollow. `p_push` changes pushes (null = keep; a new follow starts on). `p_token`: the share link you're looking through, kept with the follow so it lasts as long as that link. Returns `follow_get`. Not visible (and not following): "That's private" 42501. Your own: 22023. Unfollowing something you can no longer see works. Same values = no change, no audit row. |
| `follow_get(p_kind, p_id)` | `{following, push, unseen, last_update: {at, added, removed} \| null}`. Works on anything (never says whether it exists): not following = `{following: false, push: true, unseen: 0, last_update: null}`. |
| `follows_list()` | `{items: [Follow]}`, updated first (unseen, then newest update, then name). |
| `follow_changes(p_kind, p_id, p_limit=20)` | `{kind, id, name, updates: [Update]}` newest first, only updates since you followed. Marks them seen. Not following or not visible: 42501. |

```
Follow: {kind, id, available, push, followed_at, unseen,
         name?, format? (deck/cube), for_trade? (binder), cards?, owner?: {display_name, avatar_url},
         user_id? (only when it reaches you on their profile: open /u/<user_id>/<kind>/<id>),
         token? (only when it reaches you through the link you followed with: open /l/<token>),
         art? (a printing id, § Follow art), last_update?: {at, added, removed}}
Update: {at, added, removed, cards: [[card_id, board, delta], ...]}
```

- `art`: the printing a followed item's Collection home tile shows (§ Follow art). Absent when it holds nothing.
- `available` false: only `kind`, `id`, `available`, `push`, `followed_at`, `unseen` (0); nothing else about it.
- `cards` (Follow): the item's card count (a deck's leaves out Considering).
- `board`: `main` / `side` / `commander` for decks, `main` for cubes, `null` for binders. `delta` > 0 added, < 0
  taken out. `added` / `removed`: copies in and out (positive numbers).
- `unseen`: updates since you followed that you haven't opened (`follow_changes` marks them seen).

## How the server groups changes

- `t1_private.follow_items` (one row per followed item: kind, owner, the `snapshot` last announced, `announced_at`,
  `dirty_first` / `dirty_last`). Made on the first follow (snapshot = the item then), removed with the last one.
- Statement triggers on `card_list_entries` and `binder_items` stamp `dirty_*` on followed items only (one update
  per statement; unfollowed items cost nothing).
- `t1_private.follow_digest()` (pg_cron `t1-follow-digest`, every 5 minutes): items with `dirty_last` ≤ now − 30 min
  and `announced_at` ≤ now − 6 h (or never), up to 200 a run. Diff now vs `snapshot`; nothing net → clear the
  stamp; else write `t1_private.follow_updates` (item, at, added, removed, cards), move the snapshot, and queue one
  `follow.updated` push for the followers with pushes on who can still see it. Deleted items are skipped; updates
  older than 180 days are removed.

Push (`notify-worker`): `follow.updated`, `data: {kind, item_id, name, owner_name, added, removed, in: [names],
in_n, out: [names], out_n}` (up to 3 names each, biggest moves first; `in_n` / `out_n` = cards in / out). Title: the
item's name. Body (`follow.ts`): "Ana Lind added 12 cards and took out 8. In: Sheoldred, the Apocalypse, Fable of the
Mirror-Breaker and 10 more." Only added: "Ana Lind added 3 cards: Opt, Bolt and Ponder." Only taken out: "Ana Lind
took out 2 cards: Opt and Bolt." Collapse id `follow.updated:<item_id>`; thread `following`;
priority 5. Payload carries `follow_kind` and `item_id`. iOS only (Android push waits for FCM).

## Clients

- **Follow row** on a binder, deck or cube someone else shares with you (profile section or link screen), above
  Export: **Follow** ("Get a push when <owner> changes it"). Following: **Following** with a check, detail
  "Pushes on" / "Pushes off · changes show in Following"; tap → Table 1 menu: **Turn pushes off / on**, **Unfollow**. While
  following and there are updates: a **Changes** row ("+12 −8 · 2 h ago", "N new" in accent while unseen) → the
  Changes screen. Not on your own items, and not signed out (web link page: "Sign in to follow").
- **Library home:** a **Following** row, shown once you follow anything: "<n> followed · <m> updated" → Following.
- **Following screen:** one row per follow: name, detail "<Kind> · <owner> · +12 −8 · 2 h ago" (or "No changes
  yet"; "· pushes off" when off), an accent "N new". Tap → the item (profile section or link screen, which shows its Changes row). Long press
  / More → Turn pushes off/on, Unfollow. Unavailable rows read "A <kind> you followed" / "This isn't available" and open a menu with only Unfollow. Empty:
  "Follow a friend's deck, cube or binder to see their changes here."
- **Changes screen:** header the item's name; each update is a section "2 h ago · +12 −8", then **Added** and
  **Taken out** card rows (catalog pack name and printing, the count, deck section in the detail). Tap a card →
  its card page (search scope). Opening it marks the updates seen.
- **Push taps** (`follow.updated`): open the Changes screen for that item on the Collection tab (iOS).
- **Telemetry** (no names or ids): `follow_changed` (`kind`, `following`, `push`, `from`: item / following / menu),
  `following_viewed` (`items`, `updated`), `follow_changes_viewed` (`kind`, `updates`). Screens `following`,
  `follow_changes`.
- **Routes:** web `/library/following`, `/library/following/:kind/:id`; Android `collection/following`,
  `collection/following/{kind}/{id}`; iOS `SocialRoute.following`, `.followChanges(kind, id)`.
- **Shared rules** (`testdata/follows/cases.json`, from `make_cases.py`): the detail line of a follow and the
  Changes rows (`Follows.detail`, `Follows.rows`). Client tests: iOS `FollowsTests`, web `follows.test.ts`,
  Android `FollowsTest`.
- **Demo:** iOS Debug `-T1LibraryDemo following|followchanges|followdeck`; web `/library/following?demo`; Android
  has no Library demo.

## Account deletion

Deleting your account removes your follows, and the follow rows for things you owned (they disappear from
followers' Following), plus any trade-want adds waiting about you.

## Trade wants (John, 2026-10-25)

A push when a **friend** puts cards you **want** into their for-trade binders.

- **Who:** friends only (accepted), whose For trade (or that binder) reaches friends, never across a block, never
  demo or deleted accounts, not while the owner is restricted by moderation. No follow needed.
- **What counts as new:** copies that went into a for-trade binder (added, scanned, imported, moved in from Unsorted
  or a non-trade binder, a trade confirmed), net. Taking them back out before the push cancels them; moving between
  two for-trade binders isn't new. Marking a whole binder For trade doesn't push (yet).
- **Matching:** a want for that card (any printing), that printing, or that printing and finish, with qty > 0. A want
  with a max price is met only when the copy's price at its condition (prices follow condition) is at most the max;
  unpriced copies count.
- **Grouping:** the owner's adds go out once they've been quiet for **30 minutes**; each friend hears at most once
  every **12 hours** per owner (later adds in that window don't push; Trade matches still shows them).
- **Server:** statement triggers on `binder_items` net copies per owner, binder, printing, finish and condition into
  `t1_private.trade_adds`; `t1_private.trade_want_digest()` (pg_cron `t1-trade-wants`, every 5 minutes) matches them
  and clears them; `t1_private.trade_want_notices` keeps the 12 hours.
- **Push:** `trade.wants` to one friend, `data: {user_id (the owner), owner_name, n (cards you want), names (up to 3,
  A–Z)}`. Title "Trade match", body (`follow.ts tradeWantsCopy`): "Ana Lind put 3 cards you want up for trade:
  Counterspell, Opt and Ponder." / "… a card you want …". Payload `user_id`; collapse id `trade.wants:<owner>`;
  thread `trades`; priority 5. Mutable with the `trade.wants` kind.
- **Tap:** the trade match with that person (iOS `[.social(.matches), .social(.match(user_id))]` on the Collection
  tab; web `/library/match/<user_id>`).
