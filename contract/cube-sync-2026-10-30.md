# Sync a cube with Cube Cobra (2026-10-30)

John: "add the ability to resync a cube with Cube Cobra. When user does, it gets the new data and shows what is in and
out or updated printings of cards. Then user can accept."

## What the player sees

- Every cube of your own (not a roto deck, not one shared with you) has a **Sync with Cube Cobra** row on its screen,
  under Export. Its detail is the cube's Cube Cobra id when the link is known ("cubecobra.com · lindmark"), else
  "Get the latest from a Cube Cobra link".
- The Sync screen (title "Sync with Cube Cobra", subtitle the cube's name):
  1. **Link.** With a known link it reads Cube Cobra at once. Without one (cubes imported before this, or made by hand)
     it asks for the Cube Cobra link first (field + **Check**). **Change link** goes back to the field.
  2. **Changes.** A summary row, "3 in · 2 out · 5 new printings" ("Up to date" when nothing changes, then nothing to
     accept), with "Cube Cobra · <their cube name> · N cards" as its detail. Cards Cube Cobra lists that aren't in the
     card data are named in a notice ("Not in the card data, left out: …"), as in import. Then sections, each only when
     it has cards:
     - **In**: card rows, count, detail "SET #cn" (+ finish, + "Considering" for that section).
     - **Out**: same rows, the count in the error colour.
     - **New printing or finish**: detail "OLD #1 → NEW #2 · Foil" (from → to; the finish shows only when it isn't
       non-foil on either side).
     Rows open card detail (in the cube's scope) like version history rows.
  3. **Accept changes** (BigAction) writes the synced cube and returns to the cube. **Keep as it is** goes back.
     A cube changed elsewhere since the check is refused ("This list changed since you checked. Check again.") and the
     screen offers **Check again**.
- The synced cube is one new version noted **Synced with Cube Cobra** in Version history, so it can be restored like
  any version; the next edit starts its own version. Rankings, follows and spice follow the cube's changes as they do
  for any edit.
- A new import from a Cube Cobra link remembers the link, so its first sync needs no link.
- Telemetry: `cube_sync_checked` {in, out, changed, same, missing, known_link}, `cube_sync_accepted` {in, out,
  changed}, `cube_sync_failed` {reason: read | conflict | save}.

## Rules (shared, `testdata/cube-sync/cases.json` from `make_cases.py`)

Cube Cobra's answer comes from `list-import` (unchanged: `{url}` → entries with exact printings and finishes; mainboard
→ Main, maybeboard → Considering) and resolves to printings as in import (contract/list-import-2026-10-15.md).

Per section, copies pair in three passes: same printing and finish (unchanged; the line keeps its language, English
lines first), same printing with another finish (changed), same card (oracle) in another printing (changed). The rest
is In (Cube Cobra only) or Out (the cube only); a card moved between Main and Considering is Out of one and In the
other. Within a pass, copies pair in (card id, finish, language) order.

Language: Cube Cobra has none. An unchanged line keeps its own. A changed copy takes the new printing's language when
that printing isn't English; otherwise it keeps the old line's language when that wasn't its printing's own (an
English printing kept as German stays German); otherwise English. An In copy is its printing's language.

`summary`: "N in · N out · N new printing(s)" by copies, zeros left out; "Up to date" when all three are zero.

The saved entries are the result's `entries` (wire format, contract/lists-2026-10-15.md).

## Server (migration `20261030080000_cube_sync.sql`, tests `supabase/tests/41_cube_sync_test.sql`)

- `card_lists.source_url` (https only, ≤ 300 chars); `list_json` answers (`lists_changes`, every `list_*`) carry
  `source_url` (null when unknown).
- `list_source_set(p_list_id, p_source_url)`: remembers the link (null or blank forgets it); owner only. Clients call
  it after creating a cube from a Cube Cobra link, with `list-import`'s `source_url`
  (`https://cubecobra.com/cube/list/<id>`).
- `list_sync(p_list_id, p_seq, p_entries, p_source_url?, p_note = 'Synced with Cube Cobra')`: replaces the contents
  with `p_entries` as a new version (list_write's usual cleaning and limits) and remembers the link. `p_seq` is the
  list's `seq` the client compared against; a list that changed since raises PT409 (HTTP 409) and writes nothing.
  Owner only, authenticated only.
