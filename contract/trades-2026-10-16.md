# Trades, prices, condition, scanning: client contract (2026-10-16)

Server: migrations `20261016150000_nearby_joined.sql`, `20261016160000_trades_prices.sql`. Builds on
`contract/collection-2026-10-15.md` (all collection writes still go through `collection_apply`).

## Trades
A trade is one account's record of a swap: **gives** (copies it owns) and **gets** (any printing). Each line is
`{card_id, finish, condition, lang, qty}` (qty ≥ 1, up to 500 lines a side). The other person isn't linked; `partner` is free text.
- `trade_save(p_trade_id, p_partner, p_note, p_gives, p_gets)` creates a draft (`p_trade_id` null) or replaces one. Returns the trade. Clients save about half a second after the last edit. At most 50 open drafts.
- `trade_confirm(p_trade_id, p_idempotency_key)` applies the trade to the collection as **one batch** (`source` `trade`, `source_id` = trade id): gives as negative deltas, gets as positive. The whole thing is rejected if you no longer own what you give. Returns the `collection_apply` result plus `trade`. Undo is `collection_undo(batch_id)` like any batch. Run a delta sync afterwards.
- `trades_list(p_limit)` returns drafts first, then confirmed trades (history), newest first. `trade_delete(p_trade_id)` removes one.
- Trade fields: `id, partner, note, status ('draft'|'confirmed'), gives, gets, batch_id, created_at, updated_at, confirmed_at`.

**Decks and cubes.** Lists don't reserve copies. Before confirming, a client checks each Oracle card being given:
if any list (main + sideboard, not "considering") would need more copies than you'll own after the trade, it
says which and asks: **remove them from the lists** (sideboard first, then main) or **keep them listed** (the list
shows them missing). Cancel leaves the draft.

## Prices
`card_prices_get(p_card_ids uuid[])` → `[[card_id, usd, usd_foil, usd_etched, eur, eur_foil], …]` for up to 5000 printings.
USD is TCGplayer's market price as published by Scryfall (daily); EUR is Cardmarket. Prices are near-mint; other
conditions show the same figure. Null = no market price.

## Condition
Every collection item already carries `condition` (NM, LP, MP, HP, DMG). Clients:
- Card detail: a condition picker above "Add a printing" sets the condition new copies go in as (remembered; the scanner uses it too).
- Owned copies: tap a printing to open it, then pick another condition to move one copy or all of them (one batch: `-n` old, `+n` new).

## All cards sorts
Recent (last changed), Price (most valuable single owned copy, high to low), Set (cover printing's release date, newest first, then collector number).

## Scanning
On-device only. The camera reads the card's text; the name is matched to the local catalog pack (exact name, a
double-faced card's front face, a name cut off at the frame edge, or one misread by about a letter in eight; never
below the type line; tokens and art cards aren't scanned); the set code and collector number at the bottom pick
the printing (the copyright year and a prerelease date stamp help), else the default printing. A card with no
legible English name matches by its set code and collector number alone. A card counts once its name holds steady
(0.4 s with the printing read; 0.8 s and named in 3 frames without; a frame naming nothing doesn't reset it unless
the gap passes 1 s, 0.5 s before 2026-10-26) and can't count again until it leaves the frame for a second.

Old cards and hand fixes (2026-10-26; tests `testOldPrintings`/`testOldFrameTaken`, Android `oldPrintings`/
`oldFrameTaken`): 1993–94 cards print no set, number or year. An "Illus." line with no copyright year counts as
a 1993–94 printing (4th Edition on prints "© 1995"); when nothing tells Alpha, Beta, Unlimited and Revised apart
the scan goes in as the most printed (Revised, then Unlimited, Beta, Alpha; it was Alpha, the rarest), and a foreign
old card as its language's printing (French Sol Ring → Foreign Black Border). The printing last picked by hand on
a scanned row is the guess for the next card the text can't tell apart: its set, nearest its collector number (a
stack of Beta cards, or MH2's retro fetchlands 436–440 rather than the Zendikar originals). Anything read still wins. Never call the Scryfall API per frame. Accuracy and the
photo test set: `testdata/scan/README.md`.
Cards in other languages match by their printed name (pack N lines: de es fr it pt ru ja zhs zht ko). Nobody picks
a language (2026-10-16): the camera reads like the phone (Latin and Cyrillic, English first) until text in view
names no card for 0.6 s, then reads Chinese, Japanese and Korean, stays there while cards match and goes back after
1.2 s of misses; one reader per frame, and the switch only costs frames ScanSteadiness sees nothing in. iOS runs
Vision's accurate recognizer on camera frames about 5 times a second and turns its automatic language detection on
to switch (VisionKit's DataScanner reads one language's model at a time, so it isn't used for cards). Android
switches from ML Kit's Latin recognizer to its Japanese, Chinese and Korean ones, a frame each in turn, created only
when first needed.
- Add cards: everything scanned goes in as one batch (`source` `scan`) on Done; one Undo takes it back.
- Trades: what you give matches only cards you own (and only printings you own); what you get matches any card.
- Foil can't be told from the camera: each scanned row has a finish toggle.

### Corrections and scan reports
Nobody is asked to report a bad scan; what people already do says it, and each case becomes a matcher test case.
- Tap a scanned row to change its printing (the trailing "Printing" label; "Check printing" rows most of all) →
  `wrong_printing`, sent at once.
- × deletes a row → `removed`. If a card is then added by hand within 3 minutes (collection +1 with source
  `manual`, or a trade line picked by search), that card is the answer and it goes as `wrong_card` instead; the
  same printing added back means a duplicate was deleted, and nothing is sent. Unanswered deletions go as
  `removed` after 3 minutes (labelled by hand later).
- Back out of the scanner with nothing taken, after it saw text, then add a card by hand within 3 minutes →
  `missed`. Since 2026-10-26 also after ~2 s (10 frames) of text naming no card since the last take, with those
  frames: a card it couldn't read between ones it could (old cards typed in by hand after scanning others sent
  nothing). With no card added, nothing is sent.
- Changing the finish isn't reported: the camera can't tell foil.

`scan_report` (migrations `20261016170000_scan_reports.sql`, `20261016171000_scan_reports_missed.sql`):
- `p_frames`: OCR lines of the last 6 frames up to the take (a miss: the last 6 frames with any text), oldest
  first, each top of frame first: exactly what the matcher saw. Text only, never images.
- `p_matched_card_id`, `p_matched_exact`: what the matcher picked (null for `missed`); `p_corrected_card_id`:
  the right card (null for `removed`).
- `p_platform` (`ios`/`android`), `p_app_version` ("1.2 (34)"), `p_pack_version` (catalog pack version),
  `p_owned_only` (Trades: what you give).
- Sent in the background; a failure is dropped silently and never blocks scanning. Pending deletions and misses
  live in memory only. On by default; Account → Settings → "Improve scanning" turns it off on that device
  (stored locally: iOS `scan.reports` in UserDefaults, Android `scan.reports` in the `library` preferences).
- The server resolves name/set/cn for both cards when the report arrives. Clients can't read reports; account
  deletion keeps them without the account. `testdata/scan/tools/export_reports.py` (service key) writes them
  to `testdata/scan/reports/` for the matcher accuracy tests.
