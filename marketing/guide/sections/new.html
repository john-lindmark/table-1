  • Rankings check more carefully before placing a card (13 Nov, all platforms; live on the website and Discord): a tester's Vintage top 50 still had places 35 to 46 all black. That ranking was made on an Android build from before the 7 November checks, and replaying it under them showed two more ways a run of one color could slip through.
