Foldable phones New · iPhone Duo, Galaxy Z Fold, Pixel Fold

Table 1 lays itself out from the space it actually has and where the fold is, never from which phone it's on, as Apple and Google ask. Fold, unfold, turn or half-close the phone and the screen you're on adapts in place.

Tabletop · half folded on a table
  • Life tracker: every seat stands upright on the top half, in a row, each named (the opponent first, you last; P1 to P5 at a bigger table), with the usual taps and counters. The flat half holds the round clock large, the match line ("Game 2 · 1–0") and big Record, Options and Reset buttons.
  • A card's page: the card (or your versions) alone on the top half; the bar, its set · language · condition · finish row and the − count + stepper on the flat half.
  • Running a round: while the clock runs, the top half is a big round clock with the round's name, readable across the room; pause, +5 and the matches are on the flat half.
  • The tab bar stays along the bottom, on the flat half.
Book · fold running top to bottom
  • Life tracker: the seats stacked upright on the left page, the clock and controls on the right.
  • A card's page: the card on the left, everything you do with it on the right; Details opens on the right and keeps the card in view.
  • Running a round: the round on the left, the standings on the right.
Unfolded and wide windows
  • A window at least 840 wide and 480 tall splits in two down the middle, where an inner display's crease is: a card's page and a running round (round | standings) use both halves.
  • Landscape windows put the tab bar down the side as a rail: on iPhone Duo on the edge the system uses for its own bars, otherwise the right on iPhone and the left on Android.
  • Nothing is drawn on the fold, and buttons keep a margin from it.
Staying where you were
  • Android: folding, unfolding and turning no longer restart the app's screen, so a ranking in progress, an open counter list or a half-typed form stays as it was.
  • iPhone: the app turns with the phone on a large inner display (iPhone Duo open); ordinary iPhones and the outer display stay upright.
  • Website: Chrome on a folded phone reports the fold, and the life tracker uses the same tabletop and book layouts.

Built and tested on all three platforms on 10 November against one shared set of layout cases. Checked with a simulated fold in the iPhone Simulator: the tracker in tabletop and book, a card's page and the organizer's round clock in tabletop. Not yet tried on a real foldable, the iPhone Duo simulator (it needs the iOS 27.1 simulator, not installed on the build Mac) or a folding Android emulator, so the system's fold reports, the side rail and rotation haven't been seen yet.
