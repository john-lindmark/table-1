Finding events New

Events set to Public, and your friends' events set to Friends, can be found from the Events tab without a link or a code.

  • Public, Friends, Private: the organizer's Who can find it. Public events show to everyone on Table 1, and on the website signed out. Friends events show to the organizers' friends. Private events show to nobody; you join by QR, code, link or invitation.
  • Find events on the Events tab, under your own events: a row of filters, the first five events, soonest first, and All N. The search icon in the bar opens Find, whose tabs are Events | Leagues | Boards, with a search box over names, venues, addresses and leagues. Leagues shows Running now under its search box until you type New, and the Events tab has its own Leagues section after Find events (see Leagues).
  • Filters: Format (several at once: Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Pauper, Premodern, Canadian Highlander, Limited, Cube, Other; Any clears), Where (Everywhere, or within 25, 50, 100 or 250 km), When (Any time, Today, This weekend, This week, This month) and More (All, Public or Friends events; Team events; League events; Seats left). Your picks are kept on the device.
  • Distance: picking a distance asks for your location, only then; say no and it goes back to Everywhere. Your location is used for that search and not kept.
  • Each row: a small label over the name (the league, or FRIENDS for a friends event), then a line like "SUN 29 MAR · 10:00 · MODERN · MEH GAMES · 19 KM", in your own time zone. A running event reads "NOW · ROUND 3"; a capped one shows "12/32"; IN marks events you're signed up for. A tap opens the event's page, with Sign up before round 1 and standings after.
  • Events you run, events that are over, and events by anyone you've blocked or who blocked you aren't listed. Nothing matching reads "No events match" with Clear filters.
  • Alerts: the Events list ends with an Alerts row ("OFF", or "MODERN, LIMITED · 50 KM"). Switch it on, pick Formats (none means all), an Area (Use where I am now, asked only on that tap and kept as a rough area of about 10 km; Forget area removes it) and a Distance (Anywhere, or 25 to 250 km). About ten minutes after an event that fits becomes public you get one notification, "New Modern event near you"; never for an event you run or are in, and at most five a week.

On all three platforms. Live on the server and table-1.site since 14 November; in Android bundle 29910583 (Play internal testing) and iPhone build 63 (on its way to TestFlight). On the website, table-1.site/find works signed out and lists public events. Seen on made-up events on all three; no real public event has been listed yet. Find, league and board links open in the browser for now, not in the apps.
