Answers to the common questions, and this guide, in the app. Account → Support, or table-1.site/support on the web, signed in or not.

  • Search at the top looks through every answer and the guide at once, from two letters. Results come best match first, each saying where it's from ("FAQ · Running events", "Guide · Card scanning"). Every word you type has to match the start of a word, so "scan japanese" finds the scanner's languages; small words like "how" and "the" are left out, and plurals match ("decks" finds "deck"). When nothing matches every word, it shows the closest matches instead.
  • Questions: 14 topics (Getting started, Playing in events, Running events, Life tracker, Collection and scanning, Decks and cubes, Trades, wants and buying, Rankings, Insights, Friends and sharing, Notifications, Sync and offline, Discord, Account and privacy), each with its count. A question opens its answer, then In the guide for more.
  • Guide: Everything Table 1 does lists every part of this guide. A part reads whole, by its headings; opened from a search, it scrolls to the paragraph that matched, marked with a teal line. The build and release notes, test results and dates stay out of it.
  • Still stuck: Ask in the Table 1 Discord opens the server's invite; Account and privacy writes to privacy@table-1.site.
  • It's carried in the app, so it opens with no signal. The guide inside it is refreshed each time this guide is.
