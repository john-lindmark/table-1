Trade matches New

Collection → Trade, under Matches New: who has what you want, and who wants what you have for trade.

  • They have: your wants against their for-trade copies. They want: their wants against your for-trade copies.
  • Looks only at your friends and people you've played an event with in the last 90 days, and only at what each has shared with you. Nobody else's Library is scanned; blocked and demo accounts never match.
  • One block per person: "Has 3 · wants 2" and the value. Open it to see each card, which want it meets, and "Over your max" flags. Each copy is priced in its condition, so a Heavily Played copy can come in under your max where a near-mint one wouldn't.
  • Start a trade makes the draft for you, gives from your for-trade binders and gets from theirs, ready in the trade editor.
  • Matching follows the same order as confirming a trade: an exact printing and finish first, then that printing, then any printing; near-mint copies first.
  • A notification when a friend puts up a card you want New: see Following.
