Importing and exporting lists

New deck or New cube → Import from a link or list. Paste a link or the list itself, check the preview, rename it and pick the format, then create.

SourceHow it works
Cube CobraPaste the cube's link, or its CSV export. Exact printings and foils carry over; the maybeboard goes to Considering. A cube made from a link remembers it, for Sync with Cube Cobra later (see Decks and cubes).
ArchidektPaste the deck link. Exact printings, foils, sideboard, maybeboard, commander and format carry over.
MTGGoldfishPaste the deck link.
ManaBox and other sitesPaste a public link; the page is read automatically.
MoxfieldMoxfield doesn't let other apps read its decks. In Moxfield choose Export, copy the list as text, and paste it.
Pasted text or CSVMoxfield, ManaBox, Arena and MTGO exports: "4 Lightning Bolt (2X2) 117 *F*", section headers, a blank line before the sideboard, a plain one-card-per-line cube list, or a pasted card CSV.

When a list names a set and collector number, that exact printing is used; otherwise the original printing. A foil marking carries over when that printing comes in foil, and a card whose printing only comes in foil or etched is saved that way even when the list doesn't say.

Export New

Export on each deck or cube (yours, or one a friend shares with you) opens a screen that shows the current version as text in the format you pick (Text, Arena, Moxfield or MTGO for decks; Text or Cube Cobra for cubes). Under it: To Arena copies the Arena list (Arena imports from the clipboard) and To MTGO saves the MTGO .txt file; cubes get To Cube Cobra (the CSV). Download saves the text shown as a file and Copy text copies it.

Every format is tested byte for byte against golden files on all three platforms, and every export reads back through Table 1's own importer.
