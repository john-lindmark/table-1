Friends and profiles New

  • Friends are mutual: one asks, the other accepts. Either can unfriend at any time; it's never announced.
  • Find people among those you've played with, by name or handle among profiles open to anyone, or by email. An email search never says whether an account exists. Nobody can browse all accounts.
  • Requests carry no pressure: they show Accept · Ignore and how you know the person ("3 events together"). Ignoring is quiet; friend counts are never shown to anyone else.
  • Account → Friends: requests, friends, sent requests (Cancel), Add a friend, and Blocked (Unblock).
  • Profile: avatar, name, handle, your relationship (Add friend, Requested, Accept, Friends), their record if shared, then each part of their Library they've shared with you, read-only. Tap a card to see your own copies of it. View shows their cards as a list, grid or stacks, grouped by type, color, color identity or mana value.
  • Tap a person New: a player's row in standings (teams too), the organizer's Players lists, the waitlist, your opponent at the top of your match, roto pick order (iPhone), Head-to-head and Add a friend's results open that person's profile, ready for Add friend. Where the row's own tap does something else (Add players, Rankings, Trade matches), the avatar opens the profile. Guests can't be opened, and signed out nothing is tappable. A profile only friends can open says "Only Ana Lind's friends can see their profile." under Add friend.
  • Their decks and cubes: Export one (text, Arena, MTGO, Cube Cobra, download or copy), make a deck image of a deck, or Copy to my Library: a new deck (private) or cube with the same name, format and cards, whose history says whose it was. Copies come in non-foil. The owner isn't told.
  • View your profile as You · A friend · Anyone, to see exactly what others see.
  • Their friends New: a Friends row on a friend's profile (lists show to friends by default), or any profile whose owner shares their list with you ("3 mutual" when some are your friends too; on your own, who sees it). It opens Friends · <name>: mutual friends first, then by name, each with "Mutual friend" or their handle; you're there as "You" if you're one of them. A tap opens that person's profile; a long press offers Unfriend, Block and Report. Someone shows only if their own You in friends' lists setting reaches you (see Sharing). No totals, nothing about who's hidden, never across a block.
  • Their rankings New: a Rankings section after Cubes, newest first: each ranking under what it ranks, with its label and "40/100 ranked"; a tap opens it. It shows to friends by default (their Rankings setting in Sharing decides), only rankings they share (never Only me), and a cube ranking only when you can see that cube. On your own profile it lists all of yours, the Only me ones marked "Only me", then Popular cubes New to rank (PowerLSV, LSVCube, MTGO Vintage Cube, Arena Powered Cube; see Rankings); View as shows what a friend or anyone would see.
  • Achievements New: right under the name and record, a row of small tiles that scrolls sideways. One per league season: CHAMPION (won the final), 1ST, TOP 4 or TOP 8 in a finished season, the place so far in a running one ("#4"), QUALIFIED, or event wins ("2 WINS"), over the league and season. One per format board that lists them: the rank over the format ("#14" over Modern). A champion and a first place are outlined in teal. A league tile opens that league; a board tile opens the board. On your own profile, boards that don't list you yet show your rating alone. Who sees them is the Achievements choice in Sharing.
  • Avatars: initials on a muted tile picked from your account, or a photo (take one or choose from your library). Shown on your profile and next to names in players lists and standings.
  • Add players to an event lists friends first.
