Running an event Organizers

The organizer's screen switches between three phases on its own as the event moves along.

1 · Setup
  • Players: add or remove people before round 1 (the row goes at once), show the join QR and code, join as a player yourself. Each player shows with their avatar.
  • Seating (drafts): one long table per pod, drawn upright, seats running clockwise. Seat the ends (4+ players) puts seat 1 at the head and one at the foot. The middle reads "↻ 1·3 left" and "↺ 2 right"; see who you face in round 1.
  • Pods New (Seating, drafts): the same Pods and Pairings rows as Create, saved at once (a new number of pods re-draws the seats). Randomize draws every pod and seat again. Tap a seat to Move to another pod (to its end) or a new pod, or Swap with… and tap the other player; seats close up so every pod stays 1 to n, and every move is logged. Players who join after the draw are listed as "Not seated" and go to the smallest pod at round 1, leaving your arrangement alone. Each pod's note names its left-over seat: "Seat 7 plays Pod 2's seat 7", or "Seat 7 has the bye" when the event is odd. Locked once round 1 is paired.
  • Teams (team events): randomize, move players, rename teams.
  • Details: edit the name, when and where, the cap, format, product, teams, rounds, top cut, decklists and the clock; changes save as you make them.
  • Share link New (also in More): copies or shares the event's link, a sign-up page until round 1 (see Playing in an event).
  • Sign-up notifications New: you get a notification whenever a player signs up, joins the waitlist, drops or leaves it, with the count ("Ana signed up · 12/16 players"). Not for changes you make yourself.
  • Waitlist New: the people waiting, in order, each with Let in (seats them now, past the cap) and Remove. When a player leaves, you remove one, or you raise the cap, the next in line moves up on their own and is told. Once round 1 is paired the waitlist closes ("Didn't get in").
  • Decklists New: "5/8 decklists in", and each player's row says Decklist ✓ or No decklist. List on a row opens their list to read, or to enter or fix it (for a guest, say). With decklists Required, Start waits until every list is in.
  • More in Details New: Who can find it, Description and Address (see Creating an event); Rules (Not said, Casual or Competitive) and, when decklists are on, Decklists due with a day and time, both shown at the top of the event's About ("COMPETITIVE · DECKLISTS DUE SAT 14 NOV 09:30"); and, if you run a league, League: pick the league and season, a multiplier for a bigger event (1×, 1.5×, 2×, 3×) and whether This is the final (see Leagues).
  • Teams sign up New (Teams, team events): a switch that lets players make and join their own teams until round 1. Each team then shows its code and how full it is ("2/3"). Off, you build the teams yourself as before. The player cap counts every signed-up team whole, so a team's seats are held for its members.
  • Qualified New (Players, on a league season's final you run): a row like "3 of 23 in" opens everyone who qualified, each marked WINNER or POINTS, with where they stand: IN, INVITED, DECLINED, GUEST (no account: add them by hand) or an Invite button. Invite the qualified invites everyone at once; Next in line lists the next eight on points, to pass an invitation down.
  • Start event · pair round 1, with a confirmation. Details and teams lock, and so does the round count when it's automatic.
2 · Live
  • Round: a big countdown clock with Pause and +5 min, and matches grouped as Disputed, Waiting and Results in. Tap any match to set its result.
  • Pods New (drafts with two or more pods): the Round tab shows each pod as its own block instead, its tables in order with disputes in place, under "Pod 1 · 2 of 4 in · 1 disputed" ("All in" once every result is in); matches between pods sit under Across pods at the end, and a bye under its player's pod. Playoff rounds stay one list.
  • Your match · Table N when you're playing too: your opponent, Open life tracker and Set result, right under the clock.
  • Guest matches: when neither player has an account, your phone keeps score with the life tracker and reports the result.
  • Standings: updating as results arrive (teams first for team events). With two or more pods New, each player shows their pod (P1, P2) and a filter (All · Pod 1 · Pod 2) shows one pod in overall order.
  • Players: drop a player (they're not paired again but their results still count) or reinstate them. With two or more pods New, players are listed pod by pod by seat ("Pod 1 · 8 players", "Seat 3" on each row), then Not seated; the same in Setup's Players once the draft is seated.
  • Deck photos (More on phones, the top bar on the website) to photograph any player's deck, and to turn any player's photo upright New (a tap on it: Rotate right / Rotate left).
  • The big button moves from "Waiting on 2 results" to Pair round 3 to Finish event.
  • Earlier New: a tab after Round with every earlier round, newest first; tap a match to correct its result. With pods, each round is split by pod: "Round 1 · Pod 1 · 4 matches", "Round 2 · Across pods".
  • Re-pair round N New (More; the bar on the website): pairs the running round again from the standings as they are now, with a fresh clock. It asks first and says when results already in will be cleared. A notice on the Round tab offers it when an earlier result was corrected after the round was paired ("A round 2 result changed after round 3 was paired."). Only the latest round, while it runs, with no open dispute in earlier rounds; the old pairings stay in the event's log, and players are told "Round 3 re-paired".
  • Top 8 New: after the last Swiss round the button is Cut to top 8 (it asks first), which pairs the quarterfinals by Swiss standings: 1 v 8, 4 v 5, 2 v 7, 3 v 6, the higher seed choosing to play or draw. Then Pair semifinals, Pair the final and Finish event. Playoff matches need a winner: draws can't be entered. Rounds are named Quarterfinals, Semifinals and Final, and the standings gain a Top 8 bracket with each seed and score.
  • Top 4 New: with a top 4 the button is Cut to top 4, which pairs the semifinals 1 v 4 and 2 v 3, then the final. In team sealed and Team Trios the playoff is team against team, seat against seat, drawn as a team bracket; a 2–2 split between teams of four goes to the higher seed.
  • Display New (More on phones, the bar on the website): the address of the event's display page for a TV or projector, its QR code and Copy link; Showing (Auto, Clock, Pairings or Standings) changes what every open display shows; Announcement puts up to 140 characters along the bottom of the display (Show, Clear). On Android, with a second screen connected (a cable or a wireless display), Show on that screen fills it with the display while your phone keeps the organizer screen; without one the panel says to mirror your screen or open the address on the TV. On iPhone, mirror your screen or open the address on the TV.
  • Extra time New: a match's menu on the Round tab adds +3 min, +5 min or +10 min for that table (up to an hour in all), or Take it back. The row, the display and both players show "+5:00".
  • Judge calls New: when a table calls a judge you get a notification ("Judge! Table 7"), and the call leads the Round tab ("JUDGE · TABLE 7 · BEA · 2 MIN") until you tap Done.
  • Print New (the website's bar): Pairings by table, Pairings A–Z, Standings, or Result slips, one per match with the round, table, both players, boxes for game wins and draws and a line to sign. Sized for Letter and A4.
  • Team clinched New: in a team event with two teams, once one team has more match wins than the other could reach with every match still to play (5 of 9 in a 3-on-3 over three rounds, 7 of 12 in a 4-on-4), the round shows "Team Alpha has clinched it: 5–1 in match wins with 3 matches left to play" and the big button is Finish event straight away. Matches not yet played don't count for anyone. If the round is in, Play on · pair round N keeps going instead. A dispute still has to be settled first.
3 · Results
  • A winner card (or winning team) above the final standings. With a top 8 New the order is the winner, the finalist, the semifinalists, the quarterfinalists (each by seed), then everyone else by their Swiss standing.
  • Every round's matches, newest first, split by pod in a draft with pods New. Results can still be corrected; standings and records update. The Players tab goes pod by pod too, in final order inside each.
  • Cancelled events show standings after the last completed round and the reason.

How results work

  • One player's report counts. If the opponent reports the same score it's confirmed; a different score is a dispute.
  • Results auto-confirm at round end. Disputes must be settled before the next round can be paired.
  • The organizer can set any result; every change is recorded. A correction made after the next round was paired can be followed by Re-pair on that round New.
  • Scoring follows the Magic Tournament Rules: 3 points for a win, 1 for a draw, a bye counts as a win; tiebreakers are opponents' match-win %, game-win % and opponents' game-win %, each with a 33% floor.
  • Drafts with pods New: round 1 is across each table; odd pods' last seats play each other, pod 1's against pod 2's, and only an odd event has a bye. Inside pods, later rounds pair each pod by its own standings; an odd pod's lowest-ranked player (one who can cross without a rematch) plays another pod's, and the bye, if any, goes to one of them who hasn't had one. Across pods, later rounds are ordinary Swiss. Standings are one list either way, and the top 8 cuts from it.
  • Team events: a team wins a round by winning more of its matches; teams are ordered by those round results, then total match wins. Inside each team, players are listed best first by record, then game-win %, then opponents' match-win %, each with their record ("2-0").
  • Game and match times are recorded for a future pace-of-play view.

Schedule, sign-up link, waitlist, top 8, automatic rounds, names and decklists are on all three platforms and live on the server and table-1.site since 27 October; iPhone and Android with their next builds. Older app versions still run these events, but show Finish where newer ones show Cut to top 8. The organizer's match and guest scorekeeping are on iOS; Seat the ends on iOS, Android (from its next build) and the website (since 30 October). Deck photos are on all three since 23 October. Finishing a clinched team event is live on the website and the server; the apps show it from their next builds.
