Life tracker Updated

Full screen, edge to edge, screen stays awake. With two players, your half is at the bottom and your opponent's is upside down at the top; with three to five, everyone sits around the phone. Tap the top of a number for +1, the bottom for −1; hold to repeat.

Setup New

  • Opened without an event (from Events, the life tracker shortcut or a tracker link), the tracker starts on Setup: Players 2 · 3 · 4 · 5 and Starting life 20 · 30 · 40, starting on what you picked last time.
  • Start begins a fresh game and opens the Chooser, so the table picks who goes first.
  • Continue (when a saved game has changes or recorded games) picks it up where you left it.
  • New match in Options or History goes back to Setup. An event's match never shows Setup: it's always two players at the event's life.

Three to five players New

  • Players sit clockwise from the bottom, each facing their own edge of the phone: three is one down each side and one at the bottom, four is two down each side, five is two down each side and one at the bottom. They're named P1 to P5 (two players stay You and Opponent).
  • Each seat works like a two-player half, the way its player sees it: life on their left, counters beside it, +1 on their top half, and a swipe toward themselves to open the counter picker (only Done closes it).
  • Record lights up once only one player is left and is filled in with them; otherwise it asks who won, listing every player and Draw. Games follow one another with no best of three, and History keeps the score ("P1 1 · P2 0 · P3 2").

Commander damage New

  • At 30 or 40 starting life (Commander 40, Pauper Commander 30), the counter picker lists commander damage from each opponent first ("Commander P3", or just "Commander" with one opponent), then poison and the rest. A turned-on column reads "Cmd P3".
  • Commander damage is life lost too: +1 takes a life, −1 gives it back, and Undo or Reset takes both back together. History shows it ("P1 · Commander P3 +3 → 3").
  • 21 commander damage from one opponent loses the game; that column turns red. Oathbreaker plays at 20 without commander damage.

Everywhere

  • Every tap counts New: a tap on +1 or −1 counts however far your finger slides (on iPhone the moment it touches down, on Android and the website when it lifts). A finger that rolls a long way before the hold delay doesn't start the repeat. On Android and the website a second finger on the same zone is its own tap.
  • Counters from the top strip New: pull down from the strip along the top of your box (by the small line, the edge nearest the middle bar) to turn on up to 3 counters: poison (you lose at 10), energy, experience, rad and speed (and commander damage at 30+ life). Done closes the picker New: no swipe does, so swiping up scrolls its list instead of closing it.
  • The center strip shows the round clock (teal, amber under 10 minutes, pink in overtime), your round and table, and Record · Options · Reset. Someone watching sees Options only.
  • Reset asks first, then puts everyone's life back to the starting life and every counter to 0 for this game. In a game on your own phone with games already recorded, it also offers Reset match, which clears the games too and keeps the players, starting life and counters you turned on. In an event, recorded games stay: they're the event's record.
  • Options rises from the bottom: Chooser, Roll dice, History, New match (without an event) and Leave tracker.
  • Chooser covers the screen. Everyone puts a finger down; a ring closes around each finger, and once no finger has landed or lifted for two seconds one is picked at random and fills in teal. A moment later (1.5 seconds) the tracker comes back on its own; Done leaves sooner.
  • Roll dice: pick the die (D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20) and how many (1 to 10), then Roll. The total shows large, with each die under it ("2D6 · 4 + 5"); tap the total to roll again. Your die and count are remembered.
  • History groups quick taps into one entry ("Marcus · Life −5 → 15") and lets you undo.
  • With two players, when someone hits 0 life or 10 poison Record lights up. Recording a game moves to the next one; when the match is decided the app offers to report it, for example "Report 2–1 to the event?".
  • After the result is in New (event matches): once the match's result is reported, confirmed, disputed or set by the organizer, Record reads Result in and no more games can be recorded; change the result from the event. Life and counters still work. Clearing the result (a re-paired round) opens it again. Tables on your own phone are never locked.
  • In an event, both players' phones stay in sync and the organizer can watch read-only (or keep score for a guest match).
  • Into the next round New: once your next round is paired, the tracker opens your new match in place of the old one (Back still returns where you came from); with a bye the strip says "Round 3 paired · you have a bye". It checks on opening, every 15 seconds while on screen and when you come back to the app. Organizers watching or keeping score stay on their match.

Setup, three to five players and commander damage: built and tested on all three platforms on 3 November against one shared set of cases (seats, names, what the picker lists, commander damage taken off life, who has lost, the score); no server change. Checked on the website at phone width (Setup, the Chooser, four players, a side seat's counter picker, 3 commander damage taking 3 life) and in iPhone Simulator screenshots of every table size (not tapped); Android built and tested, not yet run on a device.
