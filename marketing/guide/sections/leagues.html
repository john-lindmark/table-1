Leagues New

A league ties events together into seasons: every event gives points, the standings add them up, and the best players qualify for the season's final.

Following a league
  • On the Events tab New: a Leagues section after Find events lists the leagues you run, then the ones you follow, then leagues running now (a season that isn't over, or an event still to play), four at most, then All leagues (when there are more) or Find leagues, which open Find → Leagues. It shows on an empty Events tab too, and is hidden when there's no league to show.
  • Find → Leagues lists Yours and Following, then a search by name or region, and under it, until you type, Running now New: up to 20 running leagues you neither run nor follow, soonest next event first (on the website signed out too). A row reads "2026 · DENVER, COLORADO · NEXT MODERN OPEN".
  • The league's page opens on the standings: the season (a dropdown when there are several), your own row outlined, then rank, name and points, with a Q beside everyone who has qualified. All N opens the whole table.
  • A player's season: tap a row for their events, each with their place and points; a dot marks results that don't count toward their best. Their picture opens their profile.
  • Events: upcoming ones soonest first, then finished ones with their winner. A tap opens the event.
  • Follow in the bar; hold it, or open details, to turn the league's notifications on or off. Signing up for a league event, or finishing one, follows the league for you once; unfollowing sticks.
  • Details (the list icon): the description, region, link, organizers, and the points rule and qualification in words ("1st 12 · 2nd 10 · Top 4 8 · Top 8 6 · Top 16 3 · Others 1", "Event winners and the top 10 on points").
Points and standings
  • Points: your finish earns the points for its bracket (first, second, top 4, top 8…), or the points for playing when it's outside them; some leagues add points per match won. A bigger event can count 1.5, 2 or 3 times. The season's final earns no points.
  • Best results: a season can count only each player's best few results; the rest still show, marked as not counted.
  • Order: points, then your best finish, then your second best, and so on. Players level on all of it share a rank.
  • Qualifying: a league can send each event's winner to the final (when the winner has already qualified, the next best finisher goes instead, down to 8th; a winning team goes together), then the top players on points among the rest. You're told at once when you qualify by a win, and when the season closes if it's on points.
  • Standings follow the events' final places, so a corrected result changes them too. Guests are on the table by name.
Running a league
  • Make one from + New on the Events tab → New league New, or the + on Find → Leagues: name, region, description and a link, then the new league's page opens. You can own three. Names and descriptions are checked like other public text.
  • The organizer button on your league's page: Edit league, Seasons and Organizers.
  • Seasons: a name, start and end dates, and a points rule from a preset: Places (12 for first, 10 for second, 8 for top 4, 6 for top 8, 3 for top 16, 1 for playing), Match wins (1 a win and 1 for playing) or Custom brackets. Then how many best results count, whether winners qualify, and how many qualify on points. Close season asks first and tells followers who finished first.
  • Organizers: add up to 20 from your friends (on iPhone also from people you've played); the league always keeps an owner.
  • Add an event: on an event you run, Setup → Details → League picks the league, the season, the multiplier and whether it's the final. Private and cancelled events can't be in a league. Followers are told once about a new upcoming event.
  • The final: see Qualified under Running an event.
