Insights New

What's winning, at a glance. Its own tab between Collection and Account (in the sidebar on a computer), with the sub-tabs Constructed · Limited · Rankings above the tab bar New; the app remembers which you used last. Free for anyone signed in.

Constructed
  • Pick Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Pauper or Canadian Highlander (Canlander; Legacy and Vintage since 31 October), Big events or All events, and a window of 7, 14, 30 or 90 days (30 to start), from dropdowns New: one row of three cells (Format · Events · Window) with a small arrow, each opening its list with the current choice outlined. The format list ends in Shown formats…, which opens the switches that hide formats you don't play. A list's own screen (Meta now and the rest) has the Events and Window dropdowns it needs.
  • The format page New: Meta over time and the matchup grid come first, then In brief with the Decks · Events · Through numbers, then one row each for Meta now, Cards rising and falling, New set, Bans and unbans (Points changes in Canadian Highlander) and Big events (or Events). Each row sums its list up in one line ("Eldrazi Bloodchief Combo 17% · 25 archetypes", "Rising most: Lightning Bolt ▲ 11", "24 events · latest Modern Challenge 64") and opens it on its own screen, in full, with the pickers that list depends on; they're the same picks as the format page.
  • Meta now: each deck's share of top finishes and its change from the window before, and under it New "Win 54.2% · Conv 31% · 1st 16%": its win rate in matches against other decks (top 8 brackets and paper rounds; mirrors left out, draws count as not won; shown from 20 matches), its conversion rate (the share of its lists that made top 8, in events with a top 8, leagues left out; shown from 20 lists; on Magic Online only the top 32 lists are published, so there it's top 32 to top 8), and 1st, the share of the window's events it won.
  • Meta over time: every named deck with at least 1% of the window's decks New (as well as the eight biggest), its share day by day over the window you picked (7, 14, 30 or 90 days) New, with bans and new sets marked. Shorter windows show dates along the bottom, 90 days shows months; a deck with no results in the window drops off the chart. The chart runs the full width New: tap a deck in the row under it to light up its line, and that deck's button shows its current share. A set is marked only when it brings the format new cards, so reprint sets like Mystery Booster Commander don't show.
  • Cards: the cards played more and less (main deck or sideboard), and the newest set's cards ranked by how much they're played.
  • Bans: for each ban or unban in the last year, the decks it hit, the decks that gained, what took the banned cards' place, and how long the meta took to settle.
  • Matchups right under Meta over time: each row's win rate against each column, the columns the ten most played decks named along the top, and a row for every named deck with at least 1% of the decks and 20 matches New, plus Other at the bottom. It follows the Big events / All events and window you picked New ("Matchups · All events, last 30 days"). A pairing with fewer than 30 matches is faded: a hint, not a verdict. Tap a square for that matchup's record, shown below the grid. Overall New, the first column, is each row's win rate against every archetype in the window (its Meta now record, or the sum of its squares when the window has none), coloured like a square; tap it for "Broodscale Combo overall" with the record and its likely range. On a phone it scrolls in its own box, the deck names pinned on the left and the column names on top New.
  • Your decks first New: signed in, the grid's rows start with the archetypes you have a deck of in that format ("Your deck", or "Your 2 decks"), then those whose usual list your collection covers at 60% or more ("You have 84%", most first; the usual list is every card in at least half that archetype's lists, at its usual copies, basic lands left out), then the rest in their usual order.
  • Names players use New: Modern's Broodscale Combo, Rakdos Delirium, Azorius Chant, Esper Goryo's, Simic Neoform and Grixis Reanimator (renamed 30 October from Eldrazi Bloodchief Combo, Rakdos Moonshadow, Azorius Control, Goryo's Vengeance, Neoform and Persist).
  • The last month's big events with their top 8s.
  • Tap a deck for its share over time, record, an example list (its best recent finish at the biggest event) with Add to my decks, core and flex cards, sideboard, matchups and recent finishes with full lists. Other is broken down by colours instead, each group with its share and an example list. Tap a card for its card page (below).
  • Search cards New: the search icon at the top of Constructed opens "<Format> cards". From two letters it lists the cards played in that format in the last year, never a basic land: the exact name first, then names starting with what you typed or with a word starting with it, then anywhere in the name, then close spellings, each by how much it was played in the last 90 days, with "Main 23% · Side 0.5%" (or "Not in the last 90 days · 6 lists this year"). Nothing found says "No Modern lists played a card called that this year."
  • The card page New (from search, Cards rising and falling, or a ban study) follows the format page's Big events / All events and window, with the pickers on the page: Main · Side · Copies (copies: the average in lists that main it), Lists ("235/707"), first and last seen; Share of lists by week over a year, main and sideboard, marked where the card itself was banned, unbanned, restricted or given new points; Played in: every archetype with a list playing it, a bar of how many of its lists do ("108 of 112 lists · 3.7 main · 1.0 side"), tapping through to the archetype; and Best lists: the ten best lists that played it in the window (biggest events, then best finish, then newest), "1st · Modern Challenge 64" and "Burn · 4 main · 1 side · NOV 3", each opening the full list with Add to my decks. Open the card goes to its own page with prices and your copies.
  • A finish's list New opens like a deck shared with you: card art, grouped by type, View (list, grid, stacks, Group by, Sort), search, Export and Deck image, with Add to my decks on top and where it finished above. Tap a card for its card page.
  • Edit at the end of the format row hides the formats you don't play (one always stays). Your picks follow your account New: hidden formats and sets, and the format, events, window, set, event and players you picked, open the same way on every device (live on the website since 30 October; the apps with their next builds).
  • Canadian Highlander has Points changes where other formats have bans: each points update is marked POINTS on the charts, with a page for who it hit and who gained.
Limited
  • Pick a set on Arena, or an Arena cube run, shown by its name, such as Arena Powered Cube · Oct 2025, the event (Premier, Traditional, Pick Two or Sealed, where there's data), and All players or Top players (a 60% win rate or better over 50 games), from the Set · Event · Players dropdowns New (Event and Players only when there's a choice; the set list ends in Shown sets… to hide sets you don't draft).
  • Cards first New: Top cards leads the page, with Sort (win rate in hand, in the opening hand, improvement when drawn, average pick), Rarity (not for a cube) and Color (W U B R G, Multicolor, Colorless) dropdowns, each taking several picks New (the list stays open; a tap puts an option in or out, Any clears, Done closes; the cell reads "C · U" or "WU · Multi"), a search box, and the ten best cards for them. Each row: its place, the art of the set's own printing, the name, the mana cost in Scryfall's symbols, rarity and games in hand, and the sort's number with, for the win rates, the points above or below the format's average under it ("+4.1"). Show all N opens the rest. Art and mana costs come from the card file on your device (the page fetches it if it's missing).
  • Search New: two letters or more find the set's cards by name (starts first, accents and apostrophes ignored), whatever the filters. With no match in the set, it searches every other set's draft data on the device, up to 20, under "Not in <set> · in other sets", and a tap opens the card's page in that set.
  • Colors, several at once New: a card is kept when all its colors are picked, so White + Blue keeps white, blue and Azorius gold cards; Multicolor keeps every gold card, Colorless the colorless ones.
  • After the cards: the color pairs and the rest, In brief and Games · Average · Weeks, New in Insights, and at the very bottom New Rank this set, the page's big action, with who has ranked it under the words.
  • A card's page New opens on the card's picture in the set's printing and its mana cost, then its win rates.
  • Colour pairs against the set's average, Deck colours (every main-colour combination with its splash), and pairs week by week: the chart's scale fits the win rates shown, with the average as a dashed AVG line New.
  • Cards: win rate in hand, in the opening hand, the gain from drawing it, and how early it's taken, with rarity and colour filters (a cube has no rarities, so no rarity filter). Taken vs win rate shows which cards go later than they should.
  • Game facts: on the play and on the draw, mulligans, game length.
  • Rank this set (Rank this cube for a cube run) opens its Rankings. Sets with no 17Lands data yet lead the Set row and open on Rank this set.
  • Spoiler season New: a set still to come, or out in the last four weeks, with no 17Lands data yet leads the set row by its code (TRK for Star Trek). Picking it says when it's out, that draft data follows about three weeks after a set reaches Arena (some never do), and how many cards Scryfall has so far, with Rank this set and the New in Insights switch. The row still opens on the newest set with data. Shown sets… lists them with the other sets, so they can be hidden the same way. The row draws once it knows which sets are coming, which it remembers on your device after your first visit, so they never jump in at the front a moment later New.
  • Arena cubes, run by run New: each cube Arena runs (Powered, Arena Cube, Planar, and any other 17Lands adds) gets an entry per run, named by its month ("Arena Powered Cube · Oct 2025"), with weeks counted from that run's first draft. A later run is a new entry; the old one stays as it was.
  • New in Insights New (at the bottom of Limited): a notification when a new Constructed format, a set's first draft data, a new cube run, or a set ready to rank (within 10 days of release) arrives. Once each, to people who opened Insights in the last 60 days; the switch turns it off.
Where it comes from
  • Constructed: published Magic Online results (Challenges, Qualifiers, Showcases and Leagues), plus paper events of 32 or more players from TopDeck.gg and from Melee.gg New, a year back. Bigger events count for more: 128 or more players four times a regular event, 64 or more twice, Regional Championships and other championships four times at any size, and a top 8 finish double.
  • Melee New events come from MTG_decklistcache, an open collection of tournament results kept on GitHub and updated daily; Table 1 reads it, not Melee. Every public list, the standings and every round count (byes left out). Melee numbers its playoff rounds like Swiss ones, so a top 8 is marked when the last three rounds are a quarterfinal, semifinal and final among the top eight. A deck's record is its paper record (TopDeck.gg and Melee) when it has one, else its Magic Online top-32 record.
  • Legacy and Vintage New come from the same three sources as the other formats. Their archetype rules were written from about 1,400 Legacy and 900 Vintage Magic Online Challenge lists of September and October 2026. Some paper events are listed as Vintage when they're really 7-point Highlander or Commander, where nearly every card is a single copy; events where half or more of the decks are like that are left out, in every format but Canadian Highlander.
  • Canadian Highlander isn't played on Magic Online, so it comes from TopDeck.gg alone, from 16 players up (its events are smaller), and its screens say "Results from TopDeck.gg". Its events are small, so most windows say the numbers rest on few events.
  • Decks are named by rules for each format's known archetypes. Canadian Highlander decks are singletons that share few cards, so they're named by their colours and plan (Esper Control, Bant Midrange, Red Deck Wins, Jeskai Black), and a colour pair or shard's own name (Izzet, Naya) for the rarer ones. New groups of decks are named by Claude and checked by a person before they show.
  • Limited: 17Lands' public data, credited on every Limited screen. Its files come out about two weeks after a set reaches Arena, then keep updating. Each cube run's data is from that run; when 17Lands publishes a new file for a cube, Table 1 reads its first rows to tell whether it's the same run updated or a new one.
  • No player names, anywhere.

In brief

Every view opens with two or three short sentences, written by Claude from that view's numbers: what moved, and one deck, card or pair worth knowing. Every number in it is checked against the view's own, and when a view rests on few events or games it says so.

Live on the server and table-1.site since 25 October; on iPhone in build 26 and on Android with its first build. Built on all three platforms and checked in the iPhone Simulator and the browser on a year of real results; Android built and tested, not yet run on a device. On the first day the data is still coming in: a year of Magic Online results takes about a day to read, the constructed views fill in at the next update (every six hours), and the Arena sets appear one by one.
