Discord New

Table 1 as a bot in your Discord server: make events, sign up and drop with a button, see who's in, and post the results with a link to the event, all from the channel. The bot acts as each person's own Table 1 account, with the same rules as the apps.

Getting it
  • Anyone with Manage Server adds it from table-1.site/discord (Add to your server). It asks only to send messages and embed links; it never reads anyone's messages.
  • /link once: the bot answers you, and only you, with a link that works for 10 minutes. Open it, sign in to Table 1 (Apple, Google, email or Discord; it makes the account if you're new) and you're linked. If you sign in to Table 1 with Discord anyway, you're linked already and can skip this. /unlink undoes it, as does Account → Discord on the website.
  • /settings timezone (Manage Server) sets the zone the server's event times are typed in; everyone sees posted times in their own.
  • The Table 1 server New: table-1.site/discord and Account → Discord offer Join the Table 1 server until you're in. With your Discord account linked, the site asks Discord whether you're a member (nothing about the answer is kept); members see "You're in the Table 1 server · Open it in Discord" on /discord and nothing on Account. Signed out or not linked, the invite always shows.
Events
  • /event create: pick the format (Standard to Canadian Highlander, Other, Draft or Sealed), and if you like a name, when ("2026-11-07 18:30"), where, a player cap, top 8, the number of rounds (left out, it follows the sign-ups), a set code or a cube. For a draft, pods and pairing New (Inside pods or Across pods), as in the app. You're seated as the organizer, and the event is a real Table 1 event you also run from the app.
  • The card the bot posts shows the name, format, when (in each reader's own time zone) and where, the organizer, the players by name with "3/8", the waitlist, rounds, top cut and the join code, with Sign up and Open in Table 1; a draft with more than one pod adds a Pods line New (the split before the draw, the pods as seated after). It redraws itself New whenever the event changes, from anywhere: a sign-up or drop in the app, on the website, through the link or here; the waitlist moving up; the organizer's changes to players, cap, name, time or venue; each round; the finish. Once round 1 is paired only the link stays. If someone deletes the card, the bot stops updating it; /event post puts up a new one.
  • Sign up seats you (or puts you on the waitlist when the event is full: "You're number 2 on the waitlist"). Already in? The bot tells you so, privately, with a Drop button. A Discord message shows the same buttons to everyone, so the buttons that are yours alone live on the answers only you see: /event show and the replies to a sign-up or drop show Drop (or Leave the waitlist) when you're in, and Sign up when you're not. Drop leaves before round 1 and moves the next person up. The same as the app's sign-up link. /event signup and /event drop do the same by name.
  • /event post puts the card of an event you made in the app in the channel: pick it from your own events (the ones you run or play in, open ones first) or paste its link. A player can post an event they're in unless it's private. /event list shows the server's open events; /event show shows one card just to you.
  • /event results posts the final standings (rank, name, record and points, the winner with a trophy, the top 8 in full) with a link to the standings on Table 1. The picker offers the server's events and your own (run or played in), finished first, so an event run from the app can have its results posted here; it joins the server as they're posted. Before the event ends, the organizer can post standings so far. A draft with two or more pods puts each player's pod beside their name New ("1. Ana · P1 · 3-0 · 9 pts"). Team events New (team draft, team sealed) show each team on its own instead, with its record, points and match wins, and its players under it best first with their records.
  • Deck photos with the results New: final results (once the event is finished) are followed by every player's deck photos, in standings order: the first follow-up headed "Deck photos · Friday Modern · 8 players", each photo titled with the place, the pod in a draft with two or more pods, and the record ("1. Bea · P2 · 2-0"), its note under it. Players without a photo and photos moderation took down are left out; at most 40 photos. Standings so far never carry photos. Posting shows the photos to everyone in the channel, and Discord keeps its copy even if a photo is later removed in Table 1; a photo turned upright in Table 1 shows upright.
Cards and decks
  • /deck posts one of your decks: name, format, card counts, its Spice, the whole deck as a picture (the deck image's cards layout, drawn on the server) and Open on Table 1. The link is the same "Anyone with the link" as in Sharing, so the bot asks before it makes one, and you can stop sharing it there any time.
  • /wants and /trades post your wants or your cards for trade: the whole list (25 lines, then "and N more"), or one card picked as you type. Each line has the printing (or "any printing"), the finish, the condition for trade, and the price: the cheapest near-mint price a want takes, or a for-trade copy's price at its condition, with the list's value at market. Open on Table 1 opens the part's link; the bot asks before it makes one. Your max prices never show.
  • /card looks a card up: cost, type, cheapest TCGplayer price (and foil), how many printings, the card's picture.
  • /meta New posts Insights as a picture: matchups (the grid: every archetype with 1% of the decks against the 10 most played, each square's win rate and record, faded under 30 matches; each row's name carries its overall win rate and record, tinted like a square New), deck (one archetype, picked as you type, against each other: bars with the record and a 95% likely range, the matchups with 30+ matches first) or winrates (every archetype with 20+ matches: record, win rate and 95% range, best first, Other last; teal when the whole range is above 50%, pink when below). Choose the format (Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage New, Pauper or Canadian Highlander), All or Big events, and 7, 14, 30 or 90 days (All events and 30 days when left out). The post links to the same view in Table 1. Anyone can use it, linked or not.
  • /ranking New posts one of your cube or set rankings: pick it as you type from the ones you made ("Lindmark Cube · Summer · 50/50 ranked"; sets with their code), and show its Top 50, Top 100 or one color's list (left out, its own top 50 or 100). The post is a picture of the list, the same as the app's Top 100 image (piles of ten in rank order, name bars showing, a rank on each card, in the cube's own printings) for a top 50, a top 100 or a color, under the cube or set and the list, "Ranking by" you and your label, and how far along it is ("37 of 50 placed so far.", "The top quarter of 68 white cards.", "This ranking's final is a top 50." when you ask a top-50 ranking for 100). Open in Table 1 shows only when the ranking is shared. Only your own rankings, and posting one changes nothing on Table 1. The places come out exactly as the app shows them.
  • /help lists every command, just for you.

Who sees what

  • Everything the bot tells you about yourself is visible to you alone. The event card, the results post and a deck you post are public in the channel, because you asked for them.
  • The card shows players by their Table 1 display name, as the event already does for its players. Nobody is ever mentioned or pinged.
  • Table 1 keeps which Discord account is which Table 1 account, where it posted each card, and the server's time zone: no usernames, no server names, no messages. Deleting your Table 1 account removes the link.
  • The bot only acts on events posted in that server, and every request from Discord is signed and checked before anything happens.
