Sync and offline

  • Your collection, binders, wants, decks, cubes and trades live in your account on the server and appear on every device you sign in to.
  • Each device keeps a copy so the Library opens instantly and works offline; changes show right away and are confirmed by the server. Cards you add (by hand or by scanning) show before the server answers, and come back off if the change fails.
  • Other devices pick up only what changed since they last checked, so syncing stays quick for large collections.
  • Repeated taps or retries never double-count: every change is applied exactly once.
  • Undo reverses a whole action (an add, a scan session, an import, a binder move, a trade) in one step.
  • Back where you were New: reopening the app (after the screen slept, or from another app) brings you back to the same screen, mid-ranking included. On Android, after 30 minutes or more away it starts on Events again; the iPhone always keeps your place.
  • Lists keep their place New (Android): back from a card's page, a deck, cube, binder, All cards or a shared list opens where you left it, in any view, with opened printings and your search kept.
  • The web app loads the Library only when you open it, so players who just use events get a smaller, faster app.

With no signal New

  • What you last saw stays: the Events tab, every event you've opened (your match and the standings, or the organizer's round) and Insights keep their last answer on the device and open on it at once, then ask the server. When the server can't be reached, the screen keeps what it has and says Couldn't refresh · last updated 3 min ago (Events and Insights at the top, an event along the bottom); with nothing saved yet it shows the error with Try again.
  • Coming back refreshes: an event refreshes when the app comes to the front (or the website's tab is shown again, or the browser says it's back online), and picks up live updates again.
  • No endless Loading…: a request with no answer after 25 seconds fails, so you see the saved screen or the error (uploads get two minutes).
  • Cards you add offline are kept: an add or a scan session that can't reach the server stays on screen and waits on the device, with the Collection home reading "3 changes waiting to send", and is sent in order with the next sync (opening the app, the Collection tab, or any later change). It can't go in twice. Undo on a waiting change takes it back out. Removals and count changes still need a connection, as do trades, binder moves and wants.
  • Decks and cubes offline: a deck or cube you make, and every change to one (cards, format, a restore, a delete), is applied on the device and sent before the next sync, on iPhone, Android and the website; a deck not yet on your account says so at the top. A change the server refuses is dropped and the deck re-read from the server.
  • The website without a connection: once you've visited table-1.site, the site itself is kept by your browser, so it opens with no connection and every page shows what it has saved. A new version of the site takes over the next time you open it.

Built and tested on all three platforms on 31 October against one shared set of cases (how long ago, what a screen says, which changes wait). The website's offline load was checked in a browser with the server stopped; the iPhone and Android screens haven't been tried with the network cut, and nothing has been tried signed in. Live on table-1.site since 1 November; iPhone in build 43 (TestFlight, 1 November); Android in bundle 29892550 (built, not uploaded).
