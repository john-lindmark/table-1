Overview

The app has four tabs: Play, Cards, Insights and Account, each with a row of sub-tabs just above the tab bar New: Play is Events · Life tracker, Cards is Collection · Trade · Wants, Insights is Constructed · Limited · Rankings and Account is Profile · Records · Settings. A tab comes back to the sub-tab you left it on, even after closing the app; only Life tracker opens full screen. Play is the product: players and organizers arrive there to play or run a tournament. Cards holds the Library: every card you own, your binders and wants, your decks, your cubes and your trades. Insights shows the metagame, Arena draft data and every ranking. Account holds your profile, your record and your settings.

Events

A live card for the event you're in, invitations, upcoming events, and past events tucked away, then Find events New for public events and your friends' (the search icon opens Find: Events, Leagues and Boards), then Leagues New: yours, the ones you follow and ones running now. + New creates an event or a league New; Life opens the life tracker for the match you're playing, or a local one.

Collection

Your collection's hero art with its card count and value (tap for All cards), then rows of your Decks, Binders and Cubes to swipe through. + adds cards or makes a deck, binder or cube; the magnifier searches every card; the camera scans a pile of cards to add anywhere. Trade, Wants and Import sit along the bottom.

Insights

Constructed (what's winning in Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Pauper and Canadian Highlander) or Limited (the sets on Arena), each opening with a short written summary.

Account

Your avatar and record, Friends, Sharing, View your profile, settings such as how you track games, privacy switches, notices from Table 1, and account deletion.

The tab bar New runs along the bottom (on the website on a phone too): an icon over each tab's name, the tab you're on in white under a thin teal line, the others with icon and name in the same light grey New. It shows only on those four screens. Every screen you open from them has a back arrow on the left, and on iPhone a swipe from the left edge goes back too (the camera is the exception).

The keyboard New goes away with a tap anywhere outside the text box you're typing in, on any screen, panel or menu; a tap in the box, or on an Add used while typing names, keeps it up. On iPhone dragging a list or going back puts it away too; on Android, moving to another screen does. A screen that opens on a search puts the cursor in it once, not again when you come back to it.

Rules are enforced on the server: pairings, standings, who can see what, and every collection, binder, deck or trade change are decided there; the apps display and submit. Standings are always calculated from results, never stored. Event data can only be changed through checked server calls that record every change.
