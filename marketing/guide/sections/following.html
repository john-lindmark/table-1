Following New

Keep up with a friend's deck, cube or binder without checking it: follow it, and hear once they've finished a round of changes.

Follow
  • On any binder, deck or cube someone else shares with you (on their profile, or a link you opened), Follow sits above Export. Signed out on the website, it says to sign in.
  • Following, the row reads Following with a check; tap it to turn notifications off or on, or Unfollow. Under it, Changes shows the latest update ("+12 −8 · 2 h ago") and how many are new. What following brings shows at once, on the same screen New.
  • A cube by link New: following it also lets you rank it, and its Follow row says so ("…and rank it"). Rankings appears the moment you follow, and goes if you unfollow or the link stops.
  • You keep a follow only while you can see it. If the owner makes it private, stops the link you followed through, blocks you or deletes it, it reads "This isn't available" and sends nothing; if they share it again, it comes back. The owner is never told who follows, or how many.
  • Up to 500 follows.
Grouped changes
  • Changes are gathered, not sent one by one: an update goes out once the list has had no changes for 30 minutes, and a list sends at most one update every 6 hours (later changes wait and join the next one).
  • An update is what changed overall since the last one: a card added and taken out again doesn't count, and a run of edits that ends where it began sends nothing.
  • Decks and cubes compare each card in each section (Main, Sideboard, Commander; Considering is left out); binders compare copies of each printing. Renaming doesn't count.
  • The notification names the list and says what came in and went out, with up to three card names. Turn one follow's notifications off and its updates still show in Following.
Following and Changes
  • On the Collection tab New, each followed deck, binder or cube sits on its row after your own, with its art (the owner's hero card, else its top card by copies), the owner's name and "N new".
  • The Following screen (from a follow notification, or the sidebar on a computer) lists each follow (updated first) with whose it is, its latest change and "N new". Tap to open it; More (or a long press) turns notifications off or unfollows.
  • Changes lists each update since you followed, newest first, with Added and Taken out cards (in a deck, which section). Tap a card for its page. Updates from before you followed aren't shown.

A friend puts up a card you want

  • When a friend adds cards to a for-trade binder you can see (by hand, by scanning, by import, by moving them in, or from a trade), and they meet one of your wants, you get one notification: "Ana Lind put 3 cards you want up for trade: Counterspell, Opt and Ponder." Tapping it opens your trade match with them.
  • A want for any printing, that printing, or that printing and finish counts; a want with a max price counts only when the copy's price at its condition is at or under it.
  • Their adds go out once they've stopped adding for 30 minutes; you hear from each friend at most once every 12 hours (Trade matches still shows everything). Cards they take back out before then, or move from one trade binder to another, don't count. Marking a whole binder For trade doesn't notify yet.
  • Friends only, and never across a block.
