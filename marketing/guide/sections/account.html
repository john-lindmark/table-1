Account and sign-in

  • Sign in with Apple, Google, Discord New or email. Any of them makes an account the first time. Android New has no Apple button: Google, Discord or email there. An Apple account signs in on Android by email with the same address.
  • What Table 1 does, before you sign in New (iPhone and Android): the sign-in screen's mark sits in a short band, and under it a carousel shows six feature spots, one page each: run your own events, play your matches, your record, insights for your decks, bring your collection, rank cubes and sets, with a shot of the real screen beside the words and a row of dots under them. It moves on every six seconds until you swipe. A scanned join QR or a sign-up link waiting, the email and code steps, the name step and the permissions screen show the mark as before.
  • Discord New: Continue with Discord opens Discord's own page (in a sheet on iPhone, a browser tab on Android, the same tab on the website), where you approve Table 1, then brings you back signed in. If your Discord email is verified and matches an existing Table 1 account, you sign in to that account; otherwise you get a new one. Closing the page or pressing Cancel on it simply takes you back.
  • Google New: in the apps, Google's own sign-in sheet; on the website, Continue with Google goes straight to Google, which says "to continue to table-1.site" (and will name Table 1 once Google has verified the brand), then brings you back to the page you were on. Cancel at Google and you're back there, signed out.
  • Email is passwordless. Enter your address and you get one email from Table 1 with both a Sign in button and a one-time code. Type the code on any device, or tap the button: with the app installed it opens the app and signs you in; on a computer it signs you in on the website.
  • Asked too soon? New A sign-in email can go to an address once a minute. Ask again sooner and you go straight to the code step, with Resend counting down to when you can ask again, because the email is already on its way. If too many sign-in emails have gone out from Table 1 in the last hour, it says "Too many sign-in emails right now. Wait a few minutes, or continue with Apple or Google." (on Android: "with Google or Discord") A double tap (or Return as well as Send) sends one email.
  • The code step waits for you New: after an email is sent, leave for your mail app and come back (even if your phone closed the app, or you reloaded the page) and you're on the code step for that address, for the code's hour. Change email, signing in, or the hour passing clears it. Not there? Check spam.
  • A new email or Discord account asks for your first and last name before anything else, so organizers and opponents see a real name (Discord only shares your username). Apple and Google accounts skip this.
  • Notifications and location New (iPhone and Android): next comes one screen on why each helps (your pairings, results and invitations; events near you and pinning your venue, with only a rough area kept). Turn on shows the phone's own questions, Skip for now goes straight in. It shows once per phone, only while one of them is unanswered, and a join QR or link you opened waits behind it. Nothing else asks for either on its own; Nearby, Create event and the venue pin ask when you tap them.
  • Signing in by email with the same address as your Apple or Google account reaches the same account.
  • The Account tab has your avatar, Friends, Sharing, View your profile, and notices from Table 1 when there are any.
  • Deck photos New: under your lifetime record, every photo of a deck you played, by event, each with its Decklist row (see After the event).
  • Settings: choose whether you track games in the app (life tracker first) or elsewhere (result reporting first); turn usage and crash data and scan reports on or off.
  • Delete your account in the app or on the website. Your match history stays as "Deleted player"; your collection, binders, wants, decks, cubes, trades, friendships, sharing settings and photos are removed, and Apple sign-in access is revoked.
  • About New: at the bottom of Account, Wizards of the Coast's fan-content notice ("Table 1 is unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy. Not approved/endorsed by Wizards."), Privacy policy, and the app's version (for example "Version 0.1.0 (32)"). On the website the notice is also at the foot of the home page and on the privacy and deletion pages.
  • Terms of service New: Account has a Terms of service row under the privacy policy (iPhone from build 59, Android from bundle 29905457) that opens table-1.site/terms; the website links it from sign-in ("Continuing agrees to the Terms and Privacy policy"), the home page's foot, Discord, Buy and your profile.
  • Store review sign-in: one review address signs in with a fixed code instead of an email, so App Store and Google Play reviewers can get in. It holds sample events and a sample collection, and works only for that address.
  • Demo data appears only for a short list of test accounts, enforced by the server in any build, and never in real records.
