Links New · iPhone and Android

Every Table 1 link is a table-1.site address. Tap one and it opens in the app if you have it, and on the website if you don't. There's nothing extra to share: the join QR, a copied join or standings link, or an address copied from the website all work.

Opens in the app
  • Events, an event (organizer or player), Add players, and the life tracker.
  • Join links: the QR joins as before; a link with just the code joins with your account's name and opens the event. Already in it? It just opens. Can't join (private, finished, cancelled)? It says why.
  • Standings links: the event screen if you organize or play in it; the standings page for everyone else, and for anyone not signed in.
  • The whole Collection: All cards, Add cards, a card, decks and cubes with their stats, versions and rankings, a ranking and its screens, binders, wants, trades and trade matches.
  • Account, Friends, Sharing, and anyone's profile (a link to one part of a profile opens the profile).
  • Share links (table-1.site/l/…): what the link shares, when you're signed in. Signed out, they open on the website, which needs no account.
Stays on the website
  • The privacy policy, account deletion, and any page the app doesn't have.
  • Signing in with Apple, Google or Discord on the website keeps you on the website, and so do the Discord bot's pages (table-1.site/discord and the link it sends you to).
  • A link the app can't show opens the website instead: in a browser window inside the app on iPhone, in your browser on Android.
Good to know
  • Signed out? The app signs you in first, then opens the screen. A join QR names the event above the sign-in.
  • Installed the Android app from a website Get the app link? It opens that event the first time you open it.
  • Works for both table-1.site and www.table-1.site.
  • Back goes where it would on the website: a deck's stats lead back to the deck, a binder back to Binders. On the website, a page opened straight from a shared link has Back to the home page (a profile link: to the profile, then Account) New.

Live on iPhone from build 20 (22 October): the website sends these links to the app since that evening. Older iPhone builds open on their home screen for the new links, so update. Android has it built and tested, and it comes with the first Play build. Tested against one shared list of links; not yet tried on a phone.
