Binders and wants New

Binders say where your copies are; wants say what you're after. Both sync to every device and feed trades and trade matches.

Binders
  • Make up to 200, named as you like. A binder holds some of your copies; the rest are Unsorted.
  • Mark a binder For trade: its copies are what trades give first and what friends can match.
  • When copies leave your collection, they come out of Unsorted first, then binders not for trade, then for-trade binders.
  • Add cards in a binder: Move in a copy you own elsewhere (Unsorted first), or + New to add one straight into the binder. Scan adds the cards you scan, new, straight into the binder as one batch. Undo any of them.
  • View shows a binder's cards as a list, a grid or stacks, and can expand every card's printings.
  • Deleting a binder puts its copies back in Unsorted.
Wants
  • Want a card in any printing, a printing, or a printing in one finish, with a quantity and an optional max price.
  • The printing picker lists every printing with rarity, year, language and price, and filters by set, number or year when there are many.
  • Your max price is never shown to anyone else; it only flags matches "over your max" for you.
  • Add a want can scan: each card you scan is wanted once, any printing (phones).
  • Prices and trend New: each want shows its near-mint price (Now $12.40 for a printing, From $3.20 for any printing: the cheapest English printing and finish), and under it the change over the last 7 days: "▼ 8% this week" (in teal), "▲ 12% this week" or "Flat this week". While a card has less than a week of history it says "since yesterday" or "in 3 days". Moves under 5 cents count as flat.
Missing
  • Worked out, never stored: every card your decks and cubes ask for more copies of than you own, in any printing New, one line per card with the largest shortfall and the lists that need it. Shown in the printing the list that needs most names.
  • Basic lands never show here New: everyone has basics, so a deck's Islands are never missing, even if your collection has none. A want you add for a basic yourself still shows.
  • One tap adds a Missing card to your wants.

In a trade, cards you get that meet a want or a Missing line (in any printing of the card) are highlighted ("Want", "Missing · Burn"), and confirming the trade takes them off your wants. Undo puts them back.

Buy on TCGplayer New

  • The Buy screen New: decks and cubes (a Buy row under Add to collection, "12 to buy · $83.40"), Wants and Missing all open one screen before TCGplayer: the total large with "60 cards · 48 owned · 12 to buy"; Skip cards I own (decks and cubes, on to start, basic lands left out while on), Any printing I own counts (on: any printing, finish or language of the card covers a line, its own printing first; off: only that printing and finish), Exact printings (off: one Mass Entry cart by name; on: each printing on its own product page; on to start for Wants), Tokens (decks, off to start: the tokens the main deck, commander and sideboard make, one each in their catalog printing, always on their own pages) and Boards (decks: command zone, main deck and sideboard on to start, Considering off). Then the cards to buy by board ("Main deck · 12 to buy · 48 owned"), each with its count and "Own 2 of 4" when you have some (a tap opens the card), Price (every listed copy at its printing's near-mint market price), Owned (minus the copies you own) and Total, and Buy on TCGplayer along the bottom, which opens the cart or the menu of links as below. The options are remembered on the device for decks, cubes, Wants and Missing separately.
  • From a card New: Add to → TCGplayer on any card's page opens the version on screen (one copy or all) on its TCGplayer product page; a printing TCGplayer has no page for goes into Mass Entry by name.
  • Buy on TCGplayer (on Wants) puts every want for any printing into TCGplayer's Mass Entry, the right count of each card by name, and opens a want for a specific printing on that printing's own TCGplayer product page New (Mass Entry rejected whole carts over one exact-printing line). With more than one link a menu lists them: Mass Entry first, then each printing with its count. Buy missing on TCGplayer (above Missing from decks and cubes) does the same for what your decks and cubes are short.
  • It opens in your browser, so TCGplayer's sign-in and cart are your own. There, pick Normal or Foil and a condition for the list, tap Add to Cart, then Optimize on the cart to find the cheapest mix of sellers. TCGplayer doesn't let another app start the optimizer, so that tap is yours.
  • A very long list comes in parts ("Part 1 of 3"), each adding to the same cart: tap Buy again for the next part.
  • Prices on TCGplayer are its live listings, so they can differ from the market price your wants show.
  • Supports Table 1 New: the links go through Table 1's TCGplayer affiliate link, which sends you on to the same page; buying from it earns Table 1 a commission at no cost to you. The link names only the app (iPhone, Android or website) and the screen (a deck, a cube, a card, Wants or Missing), never you.

Price alerts New

  • Once a day (late afternoon in Europe, midday in the US) you get one notification listing the wants that got cheaper: "Counterspell is $7.50, 25% under its 30-day average." or "Lightning Bolt is $1.40, under your max of $1.50. Also cheaper: Opt and Ponder." Tapping it opens Wants.
  • Cheaper means at least 15% and at least $1 under the want's average over the last 30 days (it needs a week of prices first). Each want alerts at most once a week, and after that only when it's 5% lower than the price it last alerted at, so a card that stays low doesn't repeat.
  • Your max price: a want with a max alerts when its price falls to it or below, once; it can alert again only after the price has gone back over the max.
  • Price alerts on the Wants screen turns them on or off for your account (on to start).
  • Prices are TCGplayer's market price as Scryfall publishes it each night, near mint. Table 1 starts keeping a card's daily prices once anyone wants it, so a card you've just added shows its trend after a day, and its alerts after a week. Individual TCGplayer listings aren't read: TCGplayer doesn't give new apps access to them. If that changes, an alert for a single listing well under market can be added.
