Deck image New

A deck as one picture, laid out the way tournament sites show decklists, to post or send. On every deck (yours, or one a friend shares) tap Deck image.

  • The picture is wide (16:9, 2400 × 1350): the deck's name, "By" the player, then the date, format, deck count and sideboard count, with the Table 1 mark in the corner.
  • Cards: each card once with its count on the corner, creatures first, then spells, then lands. A line labelled Sideboard sets off the sideboard, shown as one overlapping column. Cards grow as large as the picture allows; very big decks make the picture taller instead.
  • Stacks instead lays the deck out in overlapping columns, one per group, each titled with its count.
  • Options: Cards or Stacks; order by type, mana value, color or color identity; sideboard on or off; your name on or off (a friend's deck shows the owner's name). The preview updates as you change them.
  • Phones: Save to Photos (iPhone asks once, only to add photos; Android saves to Pictures › Table 1) or Share to Messages, Discord, Files and anything else, as a JPEG named for the deck.
  • Web: Download the JPEG, Share it where the browser can, or Copy image to paste it anywhere. Your options are remembered.
  • Zoom New: tap the preview to open the picture full screen. Pinch (or scroll, on a computer) to zoom up to 8×, drag to look around, and double-tap (double-click) to jump to 3× and back. On the web, + and − zoom, 0 fits it again, and Esc closes the zoom. The top 100 (or 50) image of a cube ranking zooms the same way.
  • Hold to copy, save or share New: a long press on the preview or the zoomed picture (the deck image and a ranking's top 100 image alike) offers Copy, Save to Photos and Share. On iPhone it's the phone's own menu with the picture above it (VoiceOver lists the three as actions); on the website it's the browser's own picture menu (hold on a phone, right-click with a mouse); on Android a Table 1 menu whose Copy puts the picture on the clipboard and whose Share shows it in the share sheet. Under the preview: "Tap to zoom · hold to copy, save or share".
  • Made on your device from the card file and card images; nothing is uploaded. Considering cards are left out.

On iPhone, Android and the web; every platform sizes the cards the same way, checked against one shared set of cases. Not yet tried on a physical phone.
