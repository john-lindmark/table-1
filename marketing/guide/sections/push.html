Notifications New · iOS and Android

  • The app asks for permission on the screen after sign-in that says what notifications are for New, only when you tap Turn on. Skip it and the app doesn't ask again on its own; turn them on in iOS Settings.
  • Sign-ups and drops New (organizers): "Ana signed up · 12/16 players", "Bea joined the waitlist · 16/16 players · 3 waiting", "Cal dropped · 7/8 players", "Dee left the waitlist". From any way in or out (the link, Discord, a code, Nearby, an invitation, a guest by code), only for the player's own move, never in demo events. Each new one replaces the last for that event, and a tap opens it. There's no switch to turn them off in the apps yet.
  • Add your deck photo New: ten minutes after your last match is in, or after the event is finished, if no photo of your deck is up yet: "That was your last match in Friday Modern. Tap to add a photo of your deck." Once per event, never for a drop (you get it when the event is finished), a guest or a demo event; checked again just before it goes, so a result undone or a round paired again sends nothing. A tap opens the event's Deck photos on Take a photo / Choose from photos (in builds before that, the event).
  • Your decklist is ready New: when a deck photo of your deck has been read into a decklist: "40 cards read from your deck photo from Friday draft, 2 to check. Tap to correct it and add it to your decks." Once per photo, to the photo's player; never for a guest or a demo event. A tap opens the photo's decklist (in builds before that, the event). Turn it off like any other notification.
  • Leagues New (for leagues you follow, with notifications on, and leagues you organize; titled with the league and season): a new event ("New event: Modern Open · Modern · MeH Games", or "The final is set: …"), results ("Bea won Modern Open. Standings are updated."), qualifying ("You qualified for the final by winning Modern Open.", or "… on points."), and the end of the season ("The season is over. Bea finished first."). A tap opens the event or the league. Off per league from its Follow button.
  • New public events New: with Alerts on in Find, "New Modern event near you" about ten minutes after an event in your formats and area becomes public. Once per event, at most five a week, never for an event you run or are in.
  • Judge! New (organizers): "Judge! Table 7", with the event and who called. A tap opens the event on its live round.
  • An invitation to a final New: when a league's organizer invites the qualified, it arrives as an ordinary event invitation.
  • You're in New: when a place opens in an event you're waiting for. A top 8 round's alert says "Quarterfinals is up", "Semifinals is up" or "The final is up".
  • Alerts arrive within seconds: invitations, a new round's pairing, results reported, disputed or changed, a cancelled event, a new event from an organizer you've played with in the last 90 days (never for private events), friend requests and accepts, and notices from Table 1.
  • New in Insights New: a new format, set data, Arena cube run or set to rank, once each, for people who've opened Insights in the last 60 days. A tap opens Insights on it (or the set's Rankings). Off with the switch at the bottom of Insights → Limited; on Android it has its own group, New in Insights.
  • Price alerts New: one a day at most, when wants got meaningfully cheaper or reached your max (see Binders and wants). Off with the Price alerts switch on Wants.
  • Deck found New: a deck finder search you started has finished ("Boros Energy · 1st · Modern Challenge 32 · 2026-10-30. Tap to create it.", "Which deck?", "No list found"). A tap opens Find a deck on that search; on Android it's in the Collection group.
  • Grouped alerts New: a round of changes to a deck, cube or binder you follow, and a friend putting up cards you want, come as one alert each, not one per card. Tapping opens the Changes or the trade match.
  • Tapping an alert opens the event, Friends or Notices. Alerts older than 30 minutes are dropped rather than arriving late.
  • On Android New: the same notifications with the same wording, in four groups you can turn off one by one in Android's settings: Events (pairings, results, invitations, sign-ups; shown at the top of the screen), Collection (follows, trade wants, price alerts, cube rankings), Friends and From Table 1. A newer one about the same thing replaces the older, and a tap opens the same screen as on iPhone; notices from Table 1 open From Table 1 and a moderator's review alert opens Review (from Android's next build) New. The phone is signed up for notifications while you're signed in and taken off at sign-out.

iPhone through Apple's push service; Android through Google's Firebase since 29 October (server live that day; the app side comes with the next Android build and hasn't been tried on a phone yet). The web has no notifications.
