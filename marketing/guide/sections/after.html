After the event New

  • Deck photos (all platforms): once the event is over, everyone in it sees every deck photo and its note, and players can still add theirs: Add a deck photo at the top of their event screen until they have one New, Deck photos · Add under After the event, or More → Add a deck photo on the organizer's Results New. The player or whoever took it can edit the note, remove it, or Rotate right / Rotate left New to set a sideways photo upright; it turns for everyone and the original file stays as it was. The organizer can turn any photo New: on a player's photo the organizer's tap opens just Rotate right / Rotate left (the card says "Rotate" where an editable one says "Edit"); the note and Remove stay with the player and whoever took it. Photos show whole, not cropped. Hold a photo New for Copy, Save to Photos and Share, on the event's screen and on your account: the photo upright as shown (iPhone: the phone's own menu; website: the browser's picture menu, or right-click; Android: a Table 1 menu).
  • On your account New: Account → Deck photos ("12 photos · 5 events", under your lifetime record) lists every photo of a deck you played across events, whoever took it, headed by event (name, format, date), newest event first. Each photo's menu: Open event, Everyone's photos from this event (finished events), Rotate, the note and Remove. A photo moderation took down shows only to whoever took it; demo events only to test accounts. On all three; the website at table-1.site/me/deck-photos.
  • Redraft (finished set drafts, iPhone, Android and the website New): everyone in the event scans their cards into one pile, most valuable first, with who added each card and its market price, and the pile's total. Tap a card to take it out. The website has no camera scanner, so there cards are added by search, in the drafted set's printing when the card is in it, with a Foil switch.

Decklists from deck photos New

Every deck photo is read into a decklist, kept beside the photo. Whoever sees the photo sees its list.

  • The read: a new photo is read by itself, usually within half a minute (not in demo events). The event's set or cube helps name the cards, as a hint and not a filter: a title read whole is kept even when it's outside the set. A die on a pile of basic lands is that land's count; a squared pile without one is counted by its edges and marked to check. Cards lying sideways to the rest, off the mat or cut off at the edge are left out of the deck, and so are tokens, dice and face-down cards.
  • The Decklist row under each photo, on the event's Deck photos and on Account → Deck photos: "Reading the decklist…", then "Decklist · 40 cards", "Decklist · 40 cards · 2 to check" or "Decklist · 40 cards · checked" once someone has corrected it. "Read decklist" asks for a read when a photo has none, and "couldn't read · try again" asks again.
  • The decklist: the photo, whole and upright (tap to zoom: it's what you correct against); the count against the deck's size, large ("40 cards", "38 cards · 2 short of 40", "101 cards · 1 over 100"; 40 for Limited, 60 for Constructed, 100 for Commander and Canadian Highlander), with the set or cube it was read against under it; then Main deck, Sideboard (when there is one) and Left out (the sideways and stray cards, never counted). A line the reader wasn't sure of is marked Check, with what it saw ("pile of about 7, no die").
  • Correcting it (the photo's player, whoever took it, and the organizer): − and + on every line; tap a line for Change card (a search of every card), Looks right, Move to main deck, sideboard or left out, and Remove; Add a card at the end of the list. Changes save as you go ("Saved"). Read the photo again under the list starts over from the photo (it asks first, since it replaces the list); a photo can be read five times in all.
  • Add to my decks (anyone who sees the photo): makes a private deck from the main deck and sideboard, named after the event ("Ana Lind · Friday draft" for someone else's deck), in the event's format when it's a Constructed one. Lines with no card are left out and counted ("2 lines without a card were left out"), then Open deck. Someone who can't correct the list can still change counts and cards before adding: those changes go into their own deck, not the photo's list.
  • The player gets one notification when a new photo's list is ready (see Notifications).

Built and tested on all three platforms and the server on 13 November against one shared set of cases. Live on the server and table-1.site since 13 November; in iPhone build 62 (TestFlight) and Android bundle 29910463 (Play internal testing). The live reader read the 24 photos posted so far in 11 to 30 seconds each: 18 came to exactly 40 cards in the main deck, the others to 27 to 41 (photos cut off at the frame or shot from far away), and it agreed with an earlier independent read on about 95% of the cards.
