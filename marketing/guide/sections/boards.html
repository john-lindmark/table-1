Leaderboards New

One board per format ranks players by a rating from their matches in public and league events.

  • Find → Boards lists every board: Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Pauper, Premodern, Canadian Highlander, Limited and Cube. A row reads "124 LISTED · YOU 1612 · #14", or "YOU 1540 · 4 TO GO" before you're listed.
  • A board: your rating large, with your rank and record once you have one, then rank, name and rating for everyone listed, each with their record ("8-4-0"). A row opens that player's profile.
  • What counts: a match between two players with accounts (no byes, no guests) in a finished event that is Public or in a league and had six or more players. Rotos and events in formats Table 1 doesn't name don't count.
  • The rating: everyone starts at 1500. A win moves you up and your opponent down, more when you beat a higher-rated player; a draw counts as half. Your first ten matches on a board move it faster. A corrected result corrects the ratings too.
  • Getting listed: after 10 rated matches on a board, with one in the last year. Players with the same rating share a rank.
  • Getting off: Account → Sharing → Leaderboards, Listed or Hidden. Hidden takes you off every board and takes your board ranks off your profile for everyone else. Your results in public events still count for the people you played.
  • People you've blocked, or who blocked you, aren't in the rows you see. Signed out on the website a board shows names only.

On all three platforms. Live on the server and table-1.site since 14 November; in Android bundle 29910583 (Play internal testing) and iPhone build 63 (on its way to TestFlight).
