Library and the card catalog

Every card printed for paper play (about 37,900 cards and 110,000 printings) is downloaded once as a compact card file and kept on your device, so search, browsing, deck pages, stats and the scanner work offline and instantly. It refreshes daily from Scryfall but downloads again only when the cards actually change. The file also carries each card's printed names in ten other languages, legality, Canadian Highlander points, the mana each card makes, the tokens it creates, and (from 28 October) each printing's illustrator and frame, for the scanner.

  • New sets the hour they appear New: Scryfall's set list is read every hour, and a set released in the last month or the next two whose card count grew is read card by card straight away, then the card file is rebuilt (at most every two hours). The nightly update still brings everything else.
  • Search by name: exact matches first, then names that start with what you typed, then any match; tokens last. Add cards searches also take Scryfall's syntax (see Advanced search).
  • Search inside a screen: a deck, cube, binder, all your binders and Wants each have their own search. Every word must appear in the card's name or type line, so "jace mind" finds Jace, the Mind Sculptor and "legendary creature" finds legends.
  • Card art loads at the smallest size that looks sharp, with rounded corners at the real card proportion, including Alpha's rounder corners and the square Collectors' Edition. On Android a picture that fails to load is tried again after 1 and 3 seconds, and one left broken in the phone's cache is fetched again New.
  • Original printing by default. Wherever you add a card it starts as the card's original paper printing, for example Alpha Lightning Bolt rather than a promo. You can change it any time.
  • Foil sheen: foil copies show a light rainbow shimmer over the card image, etched copies a dimmer one, wherever the finish is known.
  • Prices: USD is TCGplayer's market price and EUR is Cardmarket's, both via Scryfall, daily. Those are near-mint prices. A copy's price follows its condition New: NM 100%, LP 90%, MP 75%, HP 60%, DMG 40% of the near-mint price, rounded to the cent. Your collection's value, All cards, card pages, binders, trades, trade matches and the import preview price each copy in its condition. Decks, cubes, wants, search results and Stats have no condition and show near mint. (Scryfall publishes no prices by condition, so these are fixed discounts.)
  • When TCGplayer has no price New: a card's price in dollars is Cardmarket's converted at the European Central Bank's daily rate (non-foil and foil; etched never), refreshed nightly. A card's page marks it "≈ $7,992.55 · from Cardmarket". A converted price under a tenth of the card's cheapest TCGplayer price is left out, since a few Cardmarket prices point at the wrong product. Collection value, trades, trade matches and the redraft pile use it; want prices and price alerts stay on TCGplayer only.
