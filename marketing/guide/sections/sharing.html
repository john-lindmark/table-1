  • The choices read Private · Friends · Anyone, where Anyone means anyone signed in to Table 1. Each one says what it does in a line underneath.
  • Widening asks first: any change that shows more to more people lists what newly shows ("Friends will see: All cards, Wants, 3 decks, 2 binders") with Show / Cancel. Narrowing applies at once.
  • When your profile caps a part, the row says so: "Set to Anyone, but only friends see it: your profile is Friends."
  • Friends New sits in its own section under Record, with both friends settings. Widening either asks first: "Friends will see: your friends.", "Anyone on Table 1 will see: you in your friends' lists." The summary at the top names "your friends" when your list reaches someone.
  • The app offers to share just once, when it helps: when you mark a binder For trade, when trade matches have nothing to work with, and after your first event (your record).
  • Anything hidden or gone reads "This isn't available", never which. A profile you can't see past its name and photo says only friends can see it, under Add friend New.

Share, and Anyone with the link New

Share is on All cards, Wants, Binders (to share For trade), and every binder, deck and cube. It opens one screen with everything about who can see that part.

  • Anyone with the link: Make a link (it asks first, saying what the link shows) and send it anywhere. Whoever opens it sees that part and nothing else on your profile, even without a Table 1 account and even if your profile is Friends or Private. Your settings stay as they are: a deck can be Private on Table 1 and still have a link.
  • One link per part. Share link and Copy link send it; New link makes a fresh one and the old one stops working; Stop sharing link turns it off at once. Deleting the deck, cube or binder, or your account, ends its link too.
  • On Table 1: the same screen shows who sees the part there (Private, Friends or Anyone) and, when it reaches someone, Send profile link, which only those people can open.
  • As a copy (decks and cubes): Export and Deck image, as on the deck's own screen.
  • "Who sees this" says Link on while a part has a live link, and so does its Share row.
  • Opening a link: the part's name, "Shared by" with the owner's name and photo, then the cards with search, View and Group by. Decks and cubes can be exported, and signed in you can Copy to my Library. A link that's off reads "This isn't available".
  • Never through a link: your handle, your total value or your max prices. If you've blocked someone (or they you), your links don't open for them while they're signed in.
  • A deck, cube or binder name behind a link is checked like any name others can see.

Who sees what is checked on the server for every request; a hidden part and one that doesn't exist look the same from outside. The per-part sharing controls and prompts are on iOS, on Android from its next build, and on the website since 30 October New (where a long press or a right-click opens a person's menu). Links are live on the server and table-1.site since 23 October (checked there signed out); in the apps with the next iPhone and Android builds.
