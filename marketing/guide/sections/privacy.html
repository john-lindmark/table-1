Privacy and telemetry

Your Library is private until you share it, and the apps send two kinds of data to help fix and improve them, both with an off switch.

Usage and crashes

Which screens and features are used (PostHog) and crash and error reports (Sentry), identified only by your account's internal id. Turn off in Account → Share usage and crash data.

Scan reports

The text the camera read when a scan needed correcting, to improve recognition. Never images. Turn off in Account → Settings → Improve scanning.

Never sent

No email, names (yours, guests' or trade partners'), event or deck names, notes, search text, join codes or record ids. No advertising id, no session replay, no tap recording.

Business numbers (events run, matches played, trades confirmed) are counted from the database itself; telemetry only shows how people get there. Development builds send nothing. On Android only crash and error reports are sent so far; usage analytics follow. Names and photos others can see are checked by Anthropic's Claude for moderation, sent without ids or emails; the privacy policy names this.
