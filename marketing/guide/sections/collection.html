Collection

The Collection tab New

  • Your collection at the top: up to two of its hero cards' art side by side (or, without heroes, your most valuable cards), with how many cards you have and what they're worth. Tap it for All cards.
  • Decks, Binders, Cubes: a row each that you swipe sideways. Each tile shows its hero card's art (or the card it holds most of), its name, and for decks the format. The arrow beside the heading opens the full list, with New deck, New binder or New cube. With none yet, the row offers to make one.
  • Followed decks, binders and cubes come after your own on their row, with the owner's name and "N new" in teal when they've changed, and their art New: the owner's hero card, else the card it holds most of that isn't a basic land. Tap one to open it. (The full Following list opens from a follow notification, or from the sidebar on a computer.)
  • + at the top: Add cards, New deck, New binder or New cube.
  • The camera New (iPhone and Android): scan a pile of cards, then Add puts them all in your collection, a deck, a cube, a binder, your wants or a trade (see Card scanning). What it added shows under the bar with Undo.
  • Search (the magnifier): search every card by name or with Scryfall syntax. Each result shows how many copies you own, and a tap opens its card page, where you can add it anywhere.
  • Search finds decks, cubes and binders New: from two letters, Decks, Cubes and Binders sections sit above the cards. Yours come first, matched on the device by every word of the search in the name or format (so they show offline). Then everyone else's you can see: decks, cubes and binders you follow (on the owner's profile or through the link you followed), friends' shared ones, and anyone's set to Anyone on a public profile, matched by name, owner's name or format; followed first, then friends', people you've played with, everyone else. Each row reads "Deck · Modern · Mara Quinn" (yours without a name, binders "Binder · For trade") with its card count; three per kind, then Show all N. Never your own twice, nothing private, nothing across a block.
  • Cards inside lists, archetypes, rankings New: a card's name (three letters or more; basic lands left out) also finds the decks, cubes and binders that hold it, everyone else's you can see and your own, with how many ("Deck · Modern · Mara Quinn · 4 Lightning Bolt"). Archetypes lists the Insights archetypes in the formats it reads whose name or format matches, most played first, with their tournament lists in the last 90 days; a tap opens the archetype's page. Rankings lists the cube and set rankings you can see (yours first, then friends'), by the cube or set, who ranked it or its label; a tap opens the ranking. Accounts a moderator has restricted don't show.
  • Trade · Wants · Import along the bottom open Trades (with your trade matches), Wants, and Import and export.
  • Each copy is tracked by printing, finish (non-foil, foil, etched), condition and language. Two foil and three non-foil copies of the same printing are separate counts, as are copies of other printings.
  • All cards lists every card you own, grouped by card ("Lightning Bolt · 3 printings · 7 copies"), as a list, a grid or stacks. Sort by Recent, Price (most valuable single copy first, in its condition; cards with no price yet come first, so they're easy to look up), Name, MV or Set.
  • Sort New (View menu, every card list): Sort by name (A to Z), Sort by mana value (spells from lowest to highest, then lands, each by name) or Sort by set (newest set first, then collector number; the printing that counts is the one shown, and a card too new for the card data goes last). Binders also have Most copies first, where they start; All cards also has Recent and Price, and its row picks the same setting. Decks, cubes and shared sections start on Name. Each kind of screen remembers its own choice on the device; decks and cubes share theirs with ones a friend shares.
  • Group by (View menu): split All cards or a binder into groups by type, color, color identity New or mana value, or show them ungrouped. The sort applies inside each group, and in stacks each group is one stack.
  • Color identity New: each combination is its own group, in this order: White, Blue, Black, Red, Green; the pairs Azorius, Dimir, Rakdos, Gruul, Selesnya, Orzhov, Izzet, Golgari, Boros, Simic; the three-color Esper, Grixis, Jund, Naya, Bant, Abzan, Jeskai, Sultai, Mardu, Temur; the four-color Not white, Not blue, Not black, Not red, Not green; Five-color; Colorless. Headings of two colors or more show the letters ("Azorius · WU"). A card's identity is Scryfall's: mana symbols in its rules text count, so Golos is Five-color. Lands have no group of their own here: Breeding Pool is Simic, a Mountain Red, Command Tower and fetch lands Colorless.
  • Printings, collapsed: a card you own in several printings or finishes is one row with a chevron; open it to see each printing and finish with its count and price. View → Expand all printings opens every card. Lists start collapsed each visit.

The card page New

Tap any card (in All cards, a deck, a cube, a binder, your wants, a friend's profile or a shared link) and its page opens: the card as large as the screen allows, one row under it, and one bar at the bottom. The bar's price is the version on screen in its finish and condition.

  • The row: Set (a grid of every printing, earliest first), Language, Condition (NM, LP, MP, HP, DMG) and Finish (only the finishes the printing has, and hidden when it has one). When your copies differ it reads MIX. A non-English printing's language is its own.
  • Picks change your copies. On a card you own, picking another set, language, condition or finish changes the copies on screen, each staying in its binder. With several copies it asks One copy or All; with several different versions it asks before changing them all. Undo puts them back. Changing the set keeps the finish if the new printing has it.
  • The bar: − count + for the copies on screen. + adds one more of that version, − takes one away; at 0 the page keeps that version so + puts it back. New cards start as the card's original printing, in the condition and language you last added in.
  • Several versions: own it in more than one printing, finish, condition or language and they show two across, each with its count. Tap one to select it (an outline in teal): the row then shows and changes only that one, and − count + count and change that version's copies New. Tap it again to go back to all of them.
  • Add a different version New: hold + (on a computer, hold or right-click it). A copy of the version on screen (the selected one, or the one you have most of) appears as its own card, dimmed and selected, with a count of one; nothing is added yet. Change its set, language, condition or finish (in a deck or cube: set, language, finish or section). While it matches a version you already have it stays dimmed; the first change that makes it new adds it, in the binder or section you're in, with Undo. Before that, − and + set how many it will add, − at one drops it, and leaving the page drops it too.
  • Where you opened it from: from a deck or cube the page counts that list's copies and its row is Set, Finish and Section (Main, Sideboard, Considering) New: no Language or Condition there, even when the deck records one (from 26 October to 1 November it had a Language cell); from a binder it counts and changes that binder's copies. From All cards, Add cards or a link it's your whole collection.
  • Match collection New (from a deck or cube): when you own the card in printings or finishes the list doesn't name, a strip over the row reads "You own N in other printings · Match collection", and the details have the same row. It opens a grid of every version you own (printing and finish, all conditions and languages together), two across, each with "Own N" and how many are picked in teal. It starts filled in: the versions the list already uses, then the ones you hold most of. Tap a tile for one more of it; once you've picked all you own of it, or every copy the list needs, a tap puts it back to none. Clear and Auto start over; a card in more than one section picks the section first. Use these printings changes the list's copies to your picks in one edit (each keeps its language, copies not picked stay as they are) with Undo; with nothing to change it reads Already matches.
  • One or all New: with more than one copy on screen, the + at the top asks One copy or All N copies first. With a version selected it's that version's copies; otherwise All adds every version on screen with its count, to whichever place you pick next.
  • Add to: the + at the top (or the bottom bar, on a card from your wants, Missing, a friend or a shared link) adds the copies you chose (one, from the bottom bar) to your Collection, a Deck (then Main deck, Sideboard or Considering), a Cube, a Binder, your Wants (any printing, this printing, or this printing and finish) or a Trade (an open one or a new one, as a get, or a give when you own it). Undo works for collection, binder, deck and cube adds.
  • Details (the list icon): the rules text (read from Scryfall when you open it; it needs a connection), price (in the copy's condition, with near mint beside it when it isn't NM: "$9.00 · LP (NM $10.00)"), type, mana cost, rarity, artist and printing. Under them: Add to collection when opened from a deck or cube, which decks and cubes use the card (warning when they ask for more than you own), your copies with where they are and Move, Add to wants, Make it a hero, and the history of the printing on screen.
  • Double-faced cards have Flip under the card; tap the card to zoom in on it.
  • Add cards: search the catalog by name or with Scryfall syntax, or scan with the camera (phones).
  • The home summary (cards, distinct cards, value) comes from the server, priced nightly.
