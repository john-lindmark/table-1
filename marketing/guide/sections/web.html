Website

  • table-1.site has a landing page, a privacy policy and an account-deletion page (the app stores require both).
  • The landing page New: under the tagline, the sign-in button and the event code, six feature spots, each its title, headline and a few sentences beside a shot of the real screen (run your own events, play your matches, your record, insights for your decks, bring your collection, rank cubes and sets), swapping sides down the page on a wide window and stacked on a phone. The same spots open the apps' sign-in screens and fill the store listings; the shots are retaken from the real screens whenever a screen changes.
  • Join by code on the web, for players without the app.
  • Icon and install New: the Table 1 mark shows in the browser tab and on an iPhone's Home Screen when the site is added there; on Chrome and Android the site can be installed as its own app.
  • On a phone New: signed in, Events · Collection · Insights · Account sit along the bottom, as in the apps, and stay there as you scroll.
  • A display for the TV New: every event has a full-screen page for a TV, a projector or a cast browser tab (the organizer's Display panel has its address and QR code). Three views: Clock (the round clock as large as fits, the round and how many results are in), Pairings (by table, and A to Z by player with their table and opponent) and Standings (teams for team events). On Auto it shows the pairings for the first five minutes of a round, then the clock, with the standings for 20 seconds every two minutes once results come in, and the standings between rounds. Long lists turn their pages every 10 seconds. Before round 1 it shows the event's name, start time and sign-up QR; after the finish, the final standings. Extra time shows beside its table, and the organizer's announcement runs along the bottom. With a keyboard: 1, 2 and 3 pick a view, A is Auto, F is full screen, the arrows turn pages. The screen stays awake while it's open. It works signed out for any event that isn't invitation-only.
  • Print New (organizers): pairings by table or A to Z, standings and result slips, on plain light paper, for Letter and A4.
  • Find, leagues and boards New: table-1.site/find, a league's page and each board open signed out too (public events, names only on leagues and boards).
  • The site serves the files that let its links open straight in the app on iPhone and Android (see Links). Every page the app has is also a page here, for people without the app.
  • The Library, friends, trade matches, cube rankings and roto run on the web too. Card scanning, the Live Activity, widgets and push notifications are phone-only.
  • Same as the apps New (30 October): the sharing safeguards and Report, From Table 1 and Review, Seat the ends, the redraft pile, Nearby and the venue pin, and demo data for test accounts. Right-click a person's row on a computer (or long-press on a phone) for Block and Report.

On a computer New

On a window at least 1024 pixels wide, once you're signed in, the site is laid out for a computer. On a phone or a narrower window it stays exactly as it was.

  • Sidebar: a sidebar on the left replaces the Events · Collection · Account tabs. It has Play (Events, Life tracker), Collection (Overview, All cards, Add cards, Binders, Wants, Decks, Cubes, Rankings, Trades, Trade matches, Import and export) and Account (Account, Friends, Sharing). The section you're in stays marked as you go deeper: a deck under Decks, a card under All cards.
  • Events: your live, invited, upcoming and past events on the left; join with a code, create an event and the life tracker on the right.
  • Running an event: on the Live round, the clock and matches sit on the left and the standings, updating as results come in, on the right. On a window under 1280 pixels they stack.
  • Playing in an event: your match and reporting on the left; seating and standings on the right.
  • Collection: the summary across the top and the sections two across. A card's page shows the card large in its own column; pick a printing to see it there.
  • Decks and cubes: in the list view each section's groups (creatures, instants, lands…) flow into columns side by side, and the deck's actions sit three across. Grid and stacks fill the wider screen, as do All cards and binders in grid or stacks.
  • See the whole card: rest the pointer on any card in a list or a stack and the full card appears beside it.
  • Keyboard: / jumps to the screen's search and Esc closes a menu or dialog. Menus open in the middle of the screen.
