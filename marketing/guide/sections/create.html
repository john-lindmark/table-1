Creating an event Organizers

One scrolling form. Give the event a name, or leave it empty and it names itself from its format and the day, for example "Draft · Duskmourn: House of Horror · Fri 17 Oct", updating if you change the format.

  • Event name New: up to 80 characters. Setup → Details can rename it any time before round 1; clearing the name goes back to the automatic one.
  • When New: Now, or Later with a day and time (up to a year ahead). The time shows on the event, on Upcoming and on its sign-up link. Nothing starts by itself: you still pair round 1.
  • Where New (optional) and a Player cap: sign-ups past the cap join a waitlist. You can still add players past the cap yourself.
  • Type: Constructed or Limited.
  • Format: Constructed offers Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Pauper, Premodern, Canadian Highlander or Other (type a name), and Team Trios New: teams of three where each seat plays its own format. A Seats row of three cells ("A · MODERN", "B · STANDARD", "C · PIONEER") picks each seat's format, changeable until round 1. Limited offers Draft, Team draft, Sealed, Team sealed and Roto.
  • Product (Limited): pick a set from a searchable list with set icons, or a cube. Your cubes from the Library come first, then recent cubes; or paste a Cube Cobra link and the name fills in. Picking a cube hides the link and name fields. A roto draws its card pool from the Library cube you pick.
  • Teams (team formats): 3 or 4 players per team.
  • Pods New (Draft): Auto (pods of up to 8) or Choose 1 to 16, never so many that a pod would have fewer than 2; the line under it says how they split for the players so far ("2 pods · 9, 8"). Pairings: Inside pods (after round 1 you play your own pod; new drafts start here) or Across pods (Swiss over everyone). Automatic rounds count the largest pod inside pods ("Auto · 3 rounds for pods of 9"), everyone across. Team drafts and rotos are one pod.
  • Automatic rounds New (Swiss events, on to start): the number of rounds follows the players signed up, "Auto · 4 rounds for 12 players": 2 players play 1 round, 3–4 play 2, 5–8 play 3, 9–16 play 4, 17–32 play 5, 33–64 play 6, 65–128 play 7, and bigger events follow the Magic Tournament Rules' table. It's fixed when round 1 is paired. Switch it off to set the rounds by hand.
  • Top cut New (Swiss events): No top cut, Top 4 or Top 8. Top 8 is for events of players and needs 9 or more; Top 4 is for those and for team sealed and Team Trios, and needs 5 or more players or teams. With fewer the event ends after Swiss. Team drafts, rotos and round robins have no cut.
  • Decklists New (Constructed): Off, Optional or Required. Players add theirs as text or from their own Table 1 decks; Required holds round 1 until every list is in.
  • Rounds (set by hand, team events and roto): 1 to 15, default 3. Rounds run 50 minutes. A switch shows or hides the clock everywhere for that event; results still auto-confirm at round end either way.
  • Pairing (roto): Round robin for 2 to 8 players, Swiss for 9 or more, changeable until round 1. Round robin sets the number of rounds itself.
  • Players: you're in by default (switch off to only run it). Add players by name; each gets a claim link to connect their account later. Friends are listed first, then people you've played with. Invite people with accounts by email, or let them join by code, QR or nearby.
  • Who can find it New: Public (everyone on Table 1 finds it in Find events, and so does the website signed out), Friends (your friends, and the friends of anyone else who runs it with you) or Private (nobody finds it: people join by QR, code, link or invitation; new events start here). This is about finding the event, not opening it: a Friends event's link still opens for whoever has it. Setup → Details can change it later, and keeps the two older choices under a line: Nearby + QR (players within 150 m can find and join it) and Invitation only.
  • Description and Address New (optional): up to 2,000 characters of plain text for what players should know (links starting https:// can be tapped), and the venue's address. Players read them under About on the event's page.

"Your cubes" is on iOS and web; Android follows in the next batch.
