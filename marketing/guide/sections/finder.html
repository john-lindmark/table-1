Deck finder Updated

New deck → Find a deck online. Describe what you want ("latest Boros Energy from a big Modern event") and pick a format.

  • An AI agent (Claude) looks in Table 1's own results first New: every Magic Online Challenge, Showcase, Qualifier and League decklist, and Melee.gg and TopDeck.gg paper events, read within the hour of being published, for Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Pauper and Canadian Highlander (the same results Insights is built on). A list found there is copied exactly as published, with its event, date, finish and the page it came from. Then the web: mtgdecks.net, melee.gg, MTGGoldfish and official MTGO results, for Commander, Premodern, a paper event that isn't in, or a particular player's list.
  • Recency first: lists from the last week, then 30 days, then 90. Among those it prefers strong finishes at large events (Pro Tour, Regional Championships, big opens, MTGO Challenges and Showcases).
  • If your request could mean different decks, it asks a short follow-up with suggested answers, and you can type your own.
  • You see the list with its event, date, placement, player and source link before creating the deck. Cards resolve to their original printings; any card it can't match is listed, never silently dropped.
  • The search runs on the server New: tap Find deck and the phone is free. Lock it, switch apps or close the tab and the search goes on; a Deck found notification brings you back to the list when it's ready (a follow-up question comes as "Which deck?", a miss as "No list found"). While you stay on the screen it fills in by itself and no notification is shown.
  • It shows its work New: under the searching notice, a Working list grows as the finder goes, the latest step marked "…": its own short remarks ("Let me check Table 1's results first."), "Checking Table 1's results: Modern, last 30 days, \"Energy\"", what it found there ("12 events with 190 decks, newest Modern Challenge 32 · 2026-10-31"), "Reading deck 169410 from Table 1's results" then "Read Boros Energy · 1st at Modern Challenge 32", "Searching the web: boros energy modern challenge" and "Reading www.mtgo.com/decklist/…". The steps stay with the search, so a search picked up again shows how it went. Open Find a deck again and a search still running, or finished in the last hour, is right there; on the website too, in a new tab. A search can take a minute or two; one that can't finish in four is called off and you're told.
  • Each account gets 20 searches a day; a second tap on the same search costs nothing.

Built and tested on all three platforms and the server on 1 November (the database at 40 checks; the agent's parts, the notification text and the screens' rules on all three against one shared set of cases; every suite passing). Live on the server and table-1.site since 1 November: the server update applied and smoke-tested there, the finder and the notification sender deployed, and a real search from John's account found the 31 October Modern Challenge's Boros Energy list in 21 seconds from Table 1's results, with the notification delivered to both his phones. iPhone and Android with their next builds; until then the apps search the old way (which still works). Not yet tried from a phone, with a locked phone, or with a search that needs the web.
